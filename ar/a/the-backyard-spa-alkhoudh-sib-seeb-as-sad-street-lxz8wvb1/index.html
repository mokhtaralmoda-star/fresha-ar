About

The Backyard Spa is your oasis of relaxation in Al Khoudh. Nestled in a serene setting, we specialize in rejuvenating treatments designed to refresh both body and mind. Our expert therapists offer a range of services, including soothing massages, revitalizing facials, and luxurious body treatments, all tailored to your individual needs. Experience our inviting atmosphere and exceptional care as you unwind and escape the everyday hustle. Indulge in a personalized retreat that feels like home. Book your appointment today!
