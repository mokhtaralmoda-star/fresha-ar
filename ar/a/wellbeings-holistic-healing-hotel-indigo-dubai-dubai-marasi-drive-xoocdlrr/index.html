نبذة

Wellbeings at Hotel Indigo’s signature treatment is the Couples Experience. Guests start with a cleansing organic scrub in a shared steam and bath, followed by the choice of a 60-minute custom massage and facial, enjoyed side by side in the intimate deluxe couple’s suite. Couples can book this experience for AED 1,900. Founded by Christian Kiefer, the spa’s techniques are designed to balance the mind, body, and spirit. Treatments range from traditional relaxation practices such as facials and massages to contemporary areas of practice like cryotherapy, IV drip therapy, sound healing, neuroli.
