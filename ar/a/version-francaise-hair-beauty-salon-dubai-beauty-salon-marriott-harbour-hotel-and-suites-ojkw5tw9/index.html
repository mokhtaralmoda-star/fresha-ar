About

Alex & Karine have created their salon concept "Version-Francaise" in 2006 in order to offer you their top quality hair care services such as Haircuts, Coloring, Highlights, Sunlight, Perms, Straightening, Extensions or Keratin treatment, state of the art French technique and using exclusively Kerastase, Redken and L'Oreal professional products.You may also enjoy manicures, pedicures, gel nails, eyelash extensions, hair removal, Mesotherapy, SRS as well as beauty treatments using GUINOT products. The French managers and hairstylists are there, ready to listen to you and meet your expectations.
