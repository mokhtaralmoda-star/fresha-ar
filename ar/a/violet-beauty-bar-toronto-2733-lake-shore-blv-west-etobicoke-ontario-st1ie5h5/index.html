Violet Beauty Bar – Sugaring & Waxing in Etobicoke. We specialize in professional full-body sugaring and waxing for women, men, teens, and pregnancy-safe clients. We focus on comfort, hygiene, and personalized care in a clean, private, and welcoming studio environment. Whether you are new to sugaring or a regular client, we are committed to making every appointment as comfortable and effective as possible.
