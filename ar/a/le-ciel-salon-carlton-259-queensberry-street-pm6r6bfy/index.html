نبذة

Le Ciel Salon, meaning "the sky" in French, is Melbourne’s leading Korean hair salon. Our modern pink and mint interior creates a warm and stylish atmosphere, making every visit a relaxing experience. All our stylists have over 10 years of experience and specialize in Korean-style perms, balayage, layered cuts, and precision haircuts. We bring the latest trends from Korea to ensure each client gets a look that suits their style and personality. Experience expert craftsmanship and top-quality service at Le Ciel Salon.
