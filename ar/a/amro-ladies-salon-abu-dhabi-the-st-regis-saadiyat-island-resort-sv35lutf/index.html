About

Welcome to Amro Ladies Salon, the luxurious beauty salon with multiple locations throughout UAE. Conveniently located in Dubai, Al Ain and Abu Dhabi, Amro Ladies Salon is the perfect spot to relax and treat yourself. We offer a wide range of luxurious, high-end hairstyles and beauty treatments; including makeup, waxing, manicures, pedicures, and facials. Merging our years of skill and experience with top quality products, our goal is to provide you with a quality service that is second to none.
