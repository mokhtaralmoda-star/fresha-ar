نبذة

Nimbus Co is a private infrared sauna and wellness studio on Bondi Road, dedicated to hot and cold therapy. Our private rooms are designed to help you disconnect from the outside world and reconnect with yourself. At the heart of Nimbus Co are our state-of-the-art full-spectrum private infrared saunas and ice baths, supporting deep relaxation, recovery and overall wellbeing. If you’re looking for a sanctuary to restore our private wellness suites offer a calm, personal space to slow down, reset and come back to self.
