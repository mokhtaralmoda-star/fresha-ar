About

Tend Salon | تند صالون offers a comprehensive range of premium beauty services to cater to your grooming needs. Specializing in manicures & pedicures, we provide meticulous care to ensure your hands & feet look & feel their best. Our skilled technicians also offer BIAB & gel extensions for stunning, long-lasting nails. For your hair, indulge in our luxurious treatments designed to nourish & rejuvenate, leaving your locks silky smooth & radiant. Whether you're looking for a simple wash & cut or a glamorous blowout, our expert stylists are dedicated to creating the perfect look for you.
