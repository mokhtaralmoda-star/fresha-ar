About

FRQNCY is a sanctuary designed for relaxation, healing, and self-care. Beyond offering top-tier hair services, the space prioritizes client well-being, ensuring they feel safe and nurtured throughout their visit. The salon integrates holistic practices such as saging rituals, alongside sustainable beauty through eco-friendly products. It’s a place where art, spirituality, and expert craftsmanship come together, offering a one-of-a-kind experience in Dubai
