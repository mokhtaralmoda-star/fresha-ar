About

At Beauty Block Spa Lounge, clients are treated to a serene retreat where self-care takes centre stage. Specialising in a range of services from meticulous nail treatments to soothing lymphatic massages, every visit promises a rejuvenating experience tailored for women. With convenient parking and a focus on comfort, this salon transforms routine beauty rituals into moments of blissful indulgence.
