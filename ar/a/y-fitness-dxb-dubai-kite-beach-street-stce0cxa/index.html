صورة 1 من 3 صورة1/3

نبذة

Y Fitness DXB is a mobile personal training service in Dubai. We don't have a studio: your coach comes to you, at home, in your building gym, villa garden, park or on the beach, anywhere in Dubai. Our team of 17 certified coaches includes male and female trainers, so you can choose who you train with. We coach weight loss, strength and muscle building, mobility, Pilates, boxing and kickboxing, calisthenics, HYROX, swimming, seniors' fitness and kids' training. Every programme starts with an assessment and is built around your goal, level and schedule. Your coach brings equipment where needed and tracks your progress session by session. Sessions run 7 days a week, 6am to 11pm. Standard packages start at 1,400 AED for 4 sessions, down to 231 AED a session, and your first session is free on Standard packages. Off-peak Lite packages start from 187 AED a session.
