About

We specialize in Russian Nails and Lashes (Nails, Brows, and Lashes). All our masters are experts from Belarus. We offer nail services such as Essentials Mani and Pedi, Gelish Mani and Pedi, Russian Mani and Pedi, and Extensions by Acrylic and Poly Gel. Our eyelash services include Classic, 2D, 3D, and 4D Mega Volume Lashes. Additionally, we provide tinting and coloring for brows, brow lamination, brow henna, and brow coloring. Be your best at She Be Your Best.
