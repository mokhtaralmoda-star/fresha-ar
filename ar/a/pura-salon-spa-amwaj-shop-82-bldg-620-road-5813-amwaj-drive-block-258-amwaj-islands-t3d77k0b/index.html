About

Pura Salon & Spa is a luxury wellness destination designed for discerning guests who value exceptional service, privacy, and refined experiences. More than a salon and spa, Pura is a sanctuary where beauty, wellness, and hospitality come together under one roof. Our curated offerings include premium hair services, advanced skincare and facials, therapeutic spa experiences, expert nail care and professional beauty services — complemented by a dedicated café to unwind before or after your visit. For complete exclusivity, our elegant VIP Suite offers a private, personalized space. We partner with world-class brands and certified, continuously trained professionals, ensuring every treatment is tailored with precision and luxury. Beauty is you, and you are beauty.
