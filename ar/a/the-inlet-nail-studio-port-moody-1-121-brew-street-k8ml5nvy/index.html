نبذة

The Inlet Nail Studio is a locally owned, full-service nail salon specializing in Manicures, Pedicures, and Gel services. We are located in the heart of Suterbrook Village in the city of Port Moody: situated in the inlet between Rocky Point and Eagle Mountain. We are proud to be a part of this rapidly growing community for almost a decade. Our relaxed environment, with sofa-style seating, is perfect for appointments to relax on your own, or with friends and family. Cleanliness is our highest priority. All of our implements are either one time use or sanitized with medical-grade sterilization.
