Nestled in the heart of Riyadh, Aurora Spa & Wellness transports you to a tropical haven where sustainable design meets elevated luxury. This men-only sanctuary blends lush greenery with soothing Balinese rituals to offer a holistic path to well-being. In 2024, Aurora earned global recognition at the World Luxury Spa Awards, sweeping three honors: Asia’s Best Detox Program, Luxury Male Spa—Middle East & North Africa, and Saudi Arabia’s Best Interior Design. Unwind amid living trees, gentle water features, and natural light while skilled therapists tailor rejuvenating massages, signature detox.
