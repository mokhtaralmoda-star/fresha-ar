نبذة

Le Social House is Laval’s premier destination for premium men’s grooming. Located at 3200 Avenue Jean-Béraud, our shop blends the classic artistry of traditional barbering with modern, upscale styling. We specialize in precision haircuts, flawless skin fades, hot towel shaves, meticulous beard grooming, and custom hair coloring. More than just a place to get a haircut, Le Social House is built on the concept of community, a welcoming, sophisticated space where you can relax, catch up, and elevate your look. Boasting a near-perfect 4.9-star rating, our team of highly skilled, passionate barbers is dedicated to providing personalized service, sharp attention to detail, and a tailored experience for every client who walks through the door. Step in, unwind, and walk out looking and feeling your absolute best.
