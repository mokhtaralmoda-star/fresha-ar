Essence Spa operates as a ladies' boutique spa and art salon aiming at medium. It offers a range of products and services; from massages and facials to nails and eyelash extensions. Since its opening in 2006, the market for spa services in What distinguishes Essence Spa from its competitors is its technical know-how and signature treatments.
