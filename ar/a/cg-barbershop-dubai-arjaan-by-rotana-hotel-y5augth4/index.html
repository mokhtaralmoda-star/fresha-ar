نبذة

Established in 2019, CG Barbershop brings classic gents’ grooming to Dubai Media City. Our experienced barbers specialise in precision haircuts, skin fades and beard grooming, tailored to each client. We also offer men’s manicures, pedicures and facials for a complete grooming experience. Expect a relaxed, premium setting and a team that takes the time to get every detail right. Complimentary valet parking is available.
