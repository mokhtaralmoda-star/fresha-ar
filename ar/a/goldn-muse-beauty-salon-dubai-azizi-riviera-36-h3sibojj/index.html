About

GoldnMuse Salon is your luxurious destination for beauty and self-care. Located in the heart of Azizi Riviera, GoldnMuse combines high-end style with a warm, welcoming ambiance, offering a range of premier services to leave you looking and feeling your best. Our skilled team of professionals specializes in everything from hair styling, coloring, hair treatments (Botox, protein), extensions to custom facials, nails, brow lamination, eyelash extensions and luxurious spa treatments.
