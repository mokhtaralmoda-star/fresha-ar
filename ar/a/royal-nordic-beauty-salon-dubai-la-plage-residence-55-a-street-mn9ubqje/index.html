About

Royal Nordic Beauty Salon is a top-class ladies' beauty salon offering a wide range of services, including expert hair care, rejuvenating massage therapies, advanced skincare treatments, and luxurious nail care. We also provide specialized services such as permanent makeup and lash enhancements, all at great prices.We are located at Jumeriah Beach Road. Our Mission is to improve health and beauty of our female clients of all ages. We invite you to indulge your soul and senses in our relaxed and refreshing atmosphere and services.
