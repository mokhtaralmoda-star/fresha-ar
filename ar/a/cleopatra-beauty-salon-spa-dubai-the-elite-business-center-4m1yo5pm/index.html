About

Cleopatra Salon & Spa is a beauty salon located in Al Barsha. We provide all hair, body and beauty services to our clients whom we care and treat as family. For 28 years, we've curated services to fit all ladies beauty needs. Our experienced staff is here for you while our salon offers great products to be sure you get the best care. We’re located in the heart of Dubai behind The Mall of Emirates, with ample parking around the area, and the facility itself exudes a relaxing ambiance that creates a perfect haven for kicking back and having all those stresses melt away.
