نبذة

Ajimi Beauty Salon LLC is a modern beauty and wellness salon offering a complete range of professional beauty and relaxation services in a comfortable and welcoming environment. Our services include haircuts, styling, hair colouring, hair extensions, tape extensions, hair treatments, hair and scalp massage, and relaxing shoulder massage. For nail care, we offer Russian manicure, classic manicure, Russian pedicure, classic pedicure, and professional nail treatments. Enjoy our comfortable back-massage chairs while having your manicure or pedicure, with a complimentary relaxing foot massage included with your mani-pedi service. We also specialize in eyelash extensions, lash lifting, eyebrow shaping and eyebrow lamination, as well as refreshing facial treatments. Our experienced team is committed to excellent service, hygiene, quality products and attention to detail. We offer professional treatments at reasonable prices, making Ajimi Beauty Salon LLC the perfect place to relax, refresh and feel your best.
