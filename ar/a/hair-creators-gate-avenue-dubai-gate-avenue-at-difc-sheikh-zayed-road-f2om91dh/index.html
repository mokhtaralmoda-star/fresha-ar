About

Hair Creators is a top hair salon located in Gate Avenue, DIFC. We offer exceptional, advanced hair treatments and coloring services. We specialize in a diverse variety hair services tailored to meet your unique needs. From vibrant hair color, balayage, and highlights to expert color correction and precision haircuts, our experienced stylists stay up-to-date with the latest trends in the hair industry. In addition to our premium hair treatments, including smoothing, Olaplex, and also enjoy our mani-pedi treatments.
