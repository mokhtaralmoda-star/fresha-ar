About

Nestled within VO2 Gym at Villaggio Mall, VO2 Beauty Bar stands out as a haven for women seeking both classic and contemporary beauty services. With a team of skilled stylists and technicians, the salon specializes in everything from vibrant hair coloring and chic cuts to meticulous nail care and artistic nail designs. Each visit promises a personalized experience, allowing clients to express their unique beauty in an inviting and luxurious atmosphere.
