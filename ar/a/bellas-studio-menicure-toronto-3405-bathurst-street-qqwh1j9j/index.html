نبذة

Bella's Studio + Menicure is a luxury beauty, wellness, and men's grooming destination for women and men. We specialize in therapeutic manicures and pedicures, face and body facials, body grooming, waxing, lash lifts, brow laminations, brow and lash tinting, and Thai massage. We also offer medical-grade laser hair removal, as well as specialized wart and nail fungus treatments, using advanced technology for safe, effective results. Bella's Studio offers personalized care, luxury service, free parking, and a welcoming space designed to help you look and feel your best.
