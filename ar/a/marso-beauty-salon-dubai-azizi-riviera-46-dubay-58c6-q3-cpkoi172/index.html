About

Marso Beauty Salon offers a wide range of services dedicated to transforming your hair into a stunning masterpiece. Specializing in extension hair color, the salon provides vibrant shades that can elevate your look, whether you're aiming for a subtle enhancement or a bold statement. In addition to hair coloring, Marso takes pride in its expert hair treatments designed to nourish and revitalize your strands, ensuring they remain healthy and glossy. Our hairstyling services cater to every occasion, from chic everyday looks to glamorous styles for special events. With professional team
