Rather than a standard spa visit, Solace Day Spa pairs a serene bath house with attentive, welcoming care throughout. Clients particularly enjoy moving between the sauna and ice bath before unwinding with a signature or deep tissue massage, while the small-group setting and thoughtful touches make the experience feel calm and restorative from beginning to end.
