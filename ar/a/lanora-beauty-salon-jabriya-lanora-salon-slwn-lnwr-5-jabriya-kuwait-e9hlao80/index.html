About

Nestled in Jabriya, Lanora Beauty Salon offers a serene escape dedicated exclusively to women. With a range of treatments from invigorating Korean head spas to the refreshing Histemo Detox Session, clients leave feeling revitalised and pampered. The salon also features convenient parking, ensuring a hassle-free visit for those seeking a tranquil beauty experience.
