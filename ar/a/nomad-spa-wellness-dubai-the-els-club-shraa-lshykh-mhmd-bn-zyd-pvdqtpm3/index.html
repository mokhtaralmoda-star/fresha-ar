About

Rejuvenate. Replenish. Reclaim. Nestled in the heart of a unique golf club in Dubai, Nomad Wellness is the sanctuary for those who prioritize wellness, relaxation and serenity. Where rejuvenation meets extraordinary treatments. Our oasis of tranquillity offers a range of services to pamper your body and soul, from soothing massages to invigorating facials. Experience the healing touch of physiotherapy and the indulgence of a Moroccan bath. Complete your journey with our expert manicure and pedicure services. Discover a world of relaxation and renewal at Nomad Wellness.
