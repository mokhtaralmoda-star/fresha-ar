نبذة

Shaxon Hair Studio is a Black- and woman-owned salon located in Southwest Edmonton, Alberta. Specializing in Afro-textured hair, the studio offers a range of services. With over 10 years of experience, Shaxon Hair Studio provides a tranquil environment where clients can relax, enjoy complimentary refreshments, and access a selection of books and movies during their visit. The salon is committed to delivering quality hair care tailored to the unique needs of each client.
