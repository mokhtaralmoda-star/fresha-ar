نبذة

S-K Hair SALON is a Classic Salon Located in Lasalle. We do Hair Treatments, Hair Cut, Braids, African Braids, American Standard Hair Cut, Asian Cuts, Hair Coloring, Perms, Women Balayage, Head Spa, Wash, Blow, Dry, Massage, Styling, Bridal and Marriage Do-Ups, Graduation Do-Ups and also Hair Consultation with our professionals to best advise on what will work best for your hair. SK Hair Salon is located around the Angrignon Mall with Maximum Parking Space and closed to many other amenities. Stop by and you can grab anything for the day after your Appointment Session.
