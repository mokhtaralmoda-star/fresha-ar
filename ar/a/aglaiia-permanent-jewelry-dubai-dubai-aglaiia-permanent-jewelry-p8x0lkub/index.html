About

Appointments Only. Welcome to Aglaiia Permanent Jewelry, the ultimate jewelry experience. We offer a unique and personalized service where you choose the perfect chain and charms to be permanently welded onto your body, creating meaningful and lasting jewelry. Whether you come alone or with loved ones, our permanent jewelry ensures a forever-lasting symbol of your unique style and memories. Book an appointment with us to transform your jewelry experience into something truly special. We are located inside the concept store Home And Soul on the 1st floor with our sister brand The Charm Bar.
