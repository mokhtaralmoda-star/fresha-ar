نبذة

G Beauty Bar is a Korean beauty and skin studio at 163 West Pender St in downtown Vancouver, a short walk from Stadium-Chinatown Station. Our services include Korean facials, back facials, dermaplaning, chemical peels, microneedling, Indiba radiofrequency treatments, Brazilian waxing, men's waxing, sugaring for men and women, lash lifts and tints, and Korean body massage. Facials and massages include calming aromatherapy. We use Dermalogica skincare. Our esthetician is a graduate of the Professional Aesthetics/Spa Therapist program at Blanche Macdonald Centre, holds the Dermalogica Expert certification from the International Dermal Institute, and has completed the Lash Lift course with Sugar Lash. Not sure which treatment is right for you? Book our free 20-minute skin consultation first. Open Monday to Saturday, 11:00 am to 8:00 pm. Closed Sunday.
