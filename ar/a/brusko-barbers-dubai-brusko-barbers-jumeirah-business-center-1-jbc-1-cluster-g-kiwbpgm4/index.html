About

Brusko Barbers is a JLT, Dubai-based concept barbershop that offers classic and edgy haircuts, clean shaves and excellent grooming services done by our professional barbers and hairstylists.We like to think of ourselves as more than just a conventional men’s hair salon, where you get a haircut or a shave and leave. We are a premier men salon that offers authentic barber experience by combing old-school service and modern styling.
