About

X Beauty Salon is a modern beauty and wellness destination offering a wide range of professional services designed to help you look and feel your best. With a welcoming atmosphere, skilled specialists, and a strong focus on personalized care, every visit is designed to be both relaxing and results driven. Our extensive menu of services includes professional hair services and treatments, with options tailored to different hair types, needs, and styles. We also offer a complete range of nail services, from classic manicures and pedicures to polished looks for every occasion. For those looking to relax and recharge, our massage services provide the perfect opportunity to unwind and take time for yourself. Our Korean Head Spa experience takes relaxation and scalp care to another level, combining specialized scalp treatments with a deeply calming and rejuvenating ritual. At X Beauty Salon, beauty and wellness come together under one roof. Whether you’re refreshing your hair, treating yourself to beautiful nails, enjoying a relaxing massage, or indulging in a Korean Head Spa experience, our goal is to make every visit feel special.
