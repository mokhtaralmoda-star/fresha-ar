نبذة

Elevate Beauty Lofts is more than a salon — it’s an escape. With a modern, minimalist vibe, the full-service Etobicoke hair and aesthetics salon is an open, airy space that’s bathed in natural light. The loft opens onto a sunny outdoor space, perfect for salon events (or hosting yours). In this serene, intimate environment, Pamela Facey and her team help clients make hair and beauty dreams come true. We believe that beauty is rooted in feeling good. Our mission is to help our clients look and feel beautiful, in a space that offers a peaceful retreat from the outside world.
