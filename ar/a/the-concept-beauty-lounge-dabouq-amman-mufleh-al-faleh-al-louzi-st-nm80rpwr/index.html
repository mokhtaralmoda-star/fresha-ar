About

At The Concept Beauty Lounge in Dabouq, beauty meets comfort in a serene environment dedicated to pampering women. With a range of specialized services including Absolut Repair Treatments and stylish haircuts, the skilled team ensures that every visit leaves clients feeling rejuvenated. This woman-owned haven also caters to families with its pet-friendly and kid-friendly amenities, making it a perfect choice for a delightful day of self-care.
