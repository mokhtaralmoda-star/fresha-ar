About

At Lyubvi Beauty Bar, nails transform into stunning works of art, seamlessly blending creativity and precision. Offering a range of specialized services, including 1.5 volume, retouches, and 5D fillings, this beauty bar is dedicated to using only organic and vegan products. With a commitment to an adults-only atmosphere and a focus on inclusivity, clients can unwind in a space that values environmental friendliness and celebrates diverse ownership.
