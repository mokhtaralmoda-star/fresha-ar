About

A modern, vibrant, and cozy organic concept salon in JLT, we strive to use the best available vegan, natural, and cruelty-free products that deliver top results. Here's why you need to try us: we have halal nail polish and other chemical-free nail services that come with a warranty. We offer vegan facials with up to five machines. Our highly efficient, formaldehyde-free hair straightening treatments do not produce any smoke or smell. Our multinational team speaks English, Russian, Arabic, French, Hindi, and other languages. Easy parking is available.
