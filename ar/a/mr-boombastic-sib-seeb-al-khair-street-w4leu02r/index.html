About

Mr. Boombastic Barbershop is more than just a grooming spot — it’s a bold, stylish experience rooted in Afro-inspired energy and modern flair. Located in the heart of the community, Mr. Boombastic delivers top-notch services in a vibrant, welcoming environment where culture meets craft. With its unique Afro-urban aesthetic, upbeat vibe, and attention to detail, the barbershop offers everything from classic beard shaves and clean fades to trendy haircuts, manicures, and pedicures — all delivered by skilled barbers and stylists who know how to keep you looking sharp...
