About

LASHY Beauty Lounge, established in 2020, is a 100% Emirati startup company based in Dubai. Our lounge specializes in providing beauty services for women, particularly in eyelash extensions. We also provide nail care services through a wide range of colours and styles, as well as many other related services. LASHY strives to provide the best products in the world in the hands of beauty experts, to provide a distinctive and unique experience that satisfies all the preferences of our valued customers at competitive prices. Customer satisfaction is our main priority.
