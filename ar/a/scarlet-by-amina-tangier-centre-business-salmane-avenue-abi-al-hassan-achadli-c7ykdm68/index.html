About

Scarlet by Amina is a modern Beauty Lounge in Tangier offering premium aesthetic and beauty treatments inspired by Canadian standards of care, hygiene, and client experience. Our space combines luxury, professionalism, and comfort in a welcoming atmosphere designed to help every client feel confident and refreshed. We specialize in services such as Microblading, Brow & Lash treatments, Advanced Facials, Microneedling, HydraFacial, Laser Hair Removal, Makeup, Waxing, Nail Care, and personalized beauty treatments tailored to each client’s needs. At Scarlet by Amina, we focus on quality products, attention to detail, and natural-looking results while delivering a high-end experience from the moment clients walk through the door.
