About

Transformative relaxation awaits at The Wellness Haven Spa & Massage Center JVC, where skilled therapists specialize in a variety of massages including aromatherapy and couple sports massage. Emphasizing holistic well-being, this sanctuary uses organic products and eco-friendly practices, ensuring a serene environment for every guest. With amenities like showers and bath towels, along with wheelchair accessibility, your comfort is prioritized as you indulge in rejuvenating treatments tailored just for you.
