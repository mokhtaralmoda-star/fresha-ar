About

Athr Spa offers a serene escape designed exclusively for women, where pampering meets sustainability. Guests can indulge in a variety of services including express pedicures, gel nail extensions, and eco-friendly manicures, all while enjoying a tranquil atmosphere enriched by organic and vegan products. With convenient amenities like parking, showers, and lockers, Athr Spa is dedicated to providing a rejuvenating experience that prioritizes both relaxation and environmental responsibility.
