Since 2017, La Poupee Beauty Center - AL AIN BRANCH has been a trusted women-only beauty destination in Al Ain and the wider UAE. We offer a complete beauty and wellness experience under one roof — professional hair services, hair colour and treatments, nail care, facials, massage and body treatments, waxing and hair removal, lashes, brows, and makeup. With over 25 years of expertise, our team of trained beauty professionals combines personalized care with warm hospitality to make every visit feel special.
