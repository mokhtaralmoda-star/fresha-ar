About

Cut Above Hair & Beauty Salon offers a unique and tranquil environment in which to relax and leave the world behind. We offer essential maintenance treatments such as manicures, pedicures, waxing, Hair services, lashes/brows and also have a wide array of treats that have been specially created to restore balance and well being. Recognised as an approved Dermalogica & Guinot skincare centre, skin is what we know and our facials and body treatments are quite simply amazing! Our professional hair stylists have been trained all over the world in renowned salons. We are experts in developing style.
