About

Founded in 2001, it is an exclusive beauty destination for all your hair, nail, and body treatments. Set in 5 different locations across Dubai, at Gladys Marina Branch. We welcome you into a soothing ambiance where you can unwind, de-stress, and get a fabulous makeover! Gladys Marina Branch is located in the Marina Diamond building, conveniently located right beside the Jumeriah Lake Towers Tram Station and the DAMAC Metro Station.
