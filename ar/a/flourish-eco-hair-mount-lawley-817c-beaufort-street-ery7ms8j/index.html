نبذة

Looking for a hairdresser in Mount Lawley, Perth who understands your hair and your approach to haircare? I specialise in lived-in blondes, foils, highlights, dimensional colour, lowlights, grey blending, grey coverage and natural reds and coppers. From soft blonde refreshes to grey blending transformations, I create natural-looking colour that grows out beautifully. My haircuts include bobs, pixies, bixies, shags, wolf cuts, razor cuts, fringes, textured cuts, curly cuts, dry cutting and trims, tailored to your hair and lifestyle, along with styling services such as blowdries and heat styled waves and curls. As an eco-conscious hair salon, I take a considered approach to products and haircare, with options for clients who prefer more natural products or have concerns about colour and product sensitivity. If you're looking for a Mount Lawley hairdresser who values beautiful, wearable hair and personalised care, I’d love to welcome you. Located within Mane Code & Co. @817C Beaufort St, Mount Lawley.
