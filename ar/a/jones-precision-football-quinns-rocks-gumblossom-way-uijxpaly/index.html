نبذة

Jones Precision Football (JPF) is dedicated to helping young footballers reach their full potential through personalised coaching sessions. Every training session is tailored to each player's individual goals, focusing on developing technical skills, game awareness, confidence, and overall performance on the pitch. We offer 1-on-1 coaching, 2-on-1 coaching, and group sessions for 3 or more players, catering to players aged from 7 to 18 years. Each session is lead by Football Australia C licensed coach, Iestyn Jones. Iestyn has over 15 years of playing experience and an additional 7 years of coaching experience within community and specialised coaching clinics. Join JPF today - Develop Your Skills. Improve Your Game. Train with Purpose.
