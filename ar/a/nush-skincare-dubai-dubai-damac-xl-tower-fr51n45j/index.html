About

Founded in New York City and now proudly serving clients in Business Bay, Dubai, Nush Skincare is a luxury clinic offering high-end, results-driven treatments in an intimate, private setting. With a strong focus on facial symmetry, non-surgical enhancements, and tailored skincare, we specialize in advanced facials, Botox, filler, and solutions for all skin concerns — from pigmentation and acne to anti-aging and sculpting. Our four-room clinic blends cutting-edge beauty technology with curated service — where every client receives a personalized experience.
