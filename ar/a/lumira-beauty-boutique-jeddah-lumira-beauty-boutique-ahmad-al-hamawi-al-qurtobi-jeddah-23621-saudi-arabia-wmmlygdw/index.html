نبذة

LÚMIRA Beauty Boutique is a premium boutique salon specializing in Russian beauty techniques and personalized beauty services. We offer expert nail, lash, and brow treatments delivered by highly skilled specialists in an intimate and welcoming environment. Our signature services include Russian manicures and pedicures, gel overlays and extensions, lash extensions, lash lifts, brow lamination, and brow shaping. We focus on precision, quality, and healthy beauty practices to create long-lasting, elegant results tailored to each client.
