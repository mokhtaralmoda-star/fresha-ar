About

Spirals – We Do Good Hair. UK stylists | Kevin.Murphy | Blonde specialists. Find us in Emirates Garden 1, Rose 2, in the heart of JVC. Spirals is a hair salon run by a senior, UK-trained team, and blondes are our speciality: highlights, balayage, toners and root colour are what we love doing most. Every colour appointment starts with a consultation, so we understand exactly the look you're after before we begin. Our highlight packages include your colour, a toner, a wash and a finishing blow-dry, so you leave with the full result, not half the job. We're rated 5.0 from over 2,400 reviews on Fresha.
