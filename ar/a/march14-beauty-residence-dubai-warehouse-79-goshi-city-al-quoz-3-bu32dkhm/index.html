About

March14 Beauty Residence is a beauty salon in Dubai created by celebrity artist Ruby Barreau, member of the Fashion and Beauty Board in Paris, who has worked backstage with the Kardashians and JLo. Our hair salon is a L'Oréal Professionnel and Kérastase studio for blonde highlights, balayage, hair colour, keratin and Kérastase treatments and blow dries. In the Nail Theatre we create handcrafted nail art, 3D nail art, gel nail extensions, BIAB, Russian and Japanese manicures and treatment pedicures on healthy, HEMA free systems. The Lashes and Brows Atelier offers eyelash extensions in classic, hybrid, volume and signature styles, lash lifts and brow lamination. Ruby's spray tan studio creates a natural looking, odourless, anti orange spray tan mixed for your undertone. It is a large residence designed to feel like home, where every client becomes part of a family, their preferences remembered and their beauty journey treated as an ongoing project.
