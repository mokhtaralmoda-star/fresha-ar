نبذة

Hi, I’m Bally. I’ve been working in skin and beauty for about 20 years, and this spa in Panorama is where my team and I get to do things properly. We take our time with every client, we don’t rush, and we won’t talk you into treatments you don’t need. Most people come to us for facials, laser hair removal or microneedling. For facials, the one we recommend most is the OxyGeneo. Your skin feels clean and fresh afterwards, and honestly, it’s a nice hour to just lie back and relax. For laser, we look at your skin and hair first, then tell you straight what to expect and roughly how many sessions you’ll need. We do small areas and full body, for women and men. Microneedling is good for acne scars, fine lines and uneven texture. It takes a few sessions and the change is gradual, but it’s real, and your skin still looks like you, just healthier. We also do threading, waxing, hair and bridal makeup. If you’re not sure what you need, just ask when you book or when you come in. If something isn’t right for your skin, we’ll tell you. See you soon, Bally
