About

The Expert Center is a premium Beauty & Wellness space located at Voco Dubai The Palm, Palm Jumeirah. Our approach brings together advanced aesthetic expertise, personalised care and carefully selected beauty rituals across Face, Body, Hair and Nails. Discover professional treatments and renowned international brands including Biologique Recherche, LAPIDEM Tokyo, ALQVIMIA, KYDRA, Tokio Inkarami and more. From customised facials and Japanese lifting techniques to body rituals, massage, hair care, colour and precision nail services, every experience is tailored to your individual needs. At The Expert Center, beauty is approached as a complete experience — with attention to detail, expert techniques and thoughtful care at every stage. Face • Body • Hair • Nails Palm Jumeirah, Dubai
