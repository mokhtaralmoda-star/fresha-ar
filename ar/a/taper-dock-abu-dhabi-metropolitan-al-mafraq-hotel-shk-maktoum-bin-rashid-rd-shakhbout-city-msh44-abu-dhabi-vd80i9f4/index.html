About

Taper Dock Men’s Barbershop where premium grooming meets exceptional service. Conveniently located inside Metropolitan Al Mafraq Hotel in Shakhbout City, Abu Dhabi, we offer a modern and relaxing environment for every gentleman. Our professional team specializes in precision haircuts, skin fades, beard grooming, hot towel shaves, hair colouring, facial treatments, and manicure & pedicure services using high-quality products and the latest techniques. Whether you’re a hotel guest, resident, or visiting Abu Dhabi, we are committed to delivering outstanding results with attention to detail, hygiene, and customer care. Free parking available for all visitors Easy access inside Metropolitan Al Mafraq Hotel Online booking available Walk-ins are welcome (subject to availability) Experience premium grooming with comfort, style, and professionalism at Taper Dock Men’s Barbershop
