About

The Backyard Spa in Boushar, Muscat, offers a variety of men’s grooming and wellness services, including massages, facials, body waxing, Moroccan baths, and hair and beard treatments. With a calm and relaxing atmosphere, the spa is a go-to spot for those looking to unwind and refresh. The services are designed to cater specifically to men, focusing on relaxation and rejuvenation. To explore their full range of treatments or to book an appointment, visit their Fresha page.
