About

A sharp cut. Time to unwind. Elejon Barbershop brings men’s grooming to Al Sufouh 1, open daily from 10 AM to 11 PM. Visit us for haircuts, fades, beard grooming and shaving, or make time for a facial, massage, manicure or pedicure. Hair colouring, treatments and grooming combinations make it easy to bring several services into one visit. Find us on Al Sarfa Street, settle into our Chesterfield lounge and choose your preferred professional. Book a quick refresh or a complete grooming session at a time that suits you.
