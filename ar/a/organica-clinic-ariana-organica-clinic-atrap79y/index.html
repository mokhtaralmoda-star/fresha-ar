About

Organica Clinic works with the belief that nature has a solution for most of the skin types and conditions . Organica Clinic uses mostly professional organic cosmetics made of fresh herbs, fruits, stem cells, vegetables, spring waters..and thus, they treat the skin with live active ingredients instead of extracts. The wide range of our products are offered for those who prefer natural skincare solutions, free of side-effects. We respect all forms of life, therefore our products were not tested on animals and they don’t use materials extracted from animals of any kind.
