نبذة

Soul & Self is a luxury salon and wellness destination offering premium beauty services, quality products and highly skilled professionals. Our services include professional hair styling, cutting, colouring and treatments, premium nail care, manicures and pedicures, relaxing spa treatments, and an authentic Moroccan Bath experience. We carefully select high-quality, professional-grade products across our hair, nail, spa and Moroccan Bath services to deliver beautiful, consistent results while maintaining the highest standards of care and hygiene. Our talented multi-ethnic team brings diverse skills, international experience and specialised expertise to every service. From a precision haircut and flawless nails to a rejuvenating spa treatment or traditional Moroccan Bath, every experience is delivered with attention to detail, professionalism and personalised care. At Soul & Self, luxury is defined by the quality of our services, the products we use and the people who deliver them.
