About

Aleph Gents Salon is a luxury men’s grooming lounge in JLT, Dubai, specializing in precision haircuts, modern skin fades, beard sculpting and premium barbering services. Our expert barbers deliver tailored grooming experiences, including express facials, hot oil hair treatments, relaxing head massages and Brazilian hair mask therapy — all designed to help you look sharp, confident and refreshed. We use high-quality organic products and provide a clean, comfortable and family-friendly environment where every client receives exceptional care and attention.
