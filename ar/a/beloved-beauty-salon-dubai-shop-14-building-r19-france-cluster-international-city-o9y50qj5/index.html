About

Beloved Beauty Salon is a friendly, ladies-only salon at Shop 14, Building R19, France Cluster, International City. We offer hair colour, keratin and nanoplastia, haircuts and blow-dries, facials and HydraFacial, nails, lashes and brows, waxing, makeup, henna and massage. Book here for instant confirmation. Hair prices are confirmed after a short consultation, as longer or thicker hair may need extra product. Open daily from 10 AM. Also available: balayage and highlights, protein hair treatment, Gelish manicure and pedicure, nail extensions, eyelash extensions, eyebrow threading and braids. Convenient for ladies in International City, Warsan, Dubai Silicon Oasis, Al Warqa and Mirdif.
