نبذة

KAYA Nail & Beauty is a boutique nail and beauty studio located in Edgemont Village, North Vancouver. Our 14-station space features 6 manicure and 6 pedicure stations plus 2 dedicated lash beds, offering gel manicures, gel pedicures, lash lifts, brow shaping, and a curated retail selection. We combine elevated design, premium products, and attentive service in a calm, welcoming setting. Whether you're booking a quick manicure or treating yourself to a full nail and lash refresh, our team is here to make every visit feel relaxed and personal. Conveniently located for North Vancouver locals, KAYA is the neighbourhood's newest destination for quality nail and beauty care.
