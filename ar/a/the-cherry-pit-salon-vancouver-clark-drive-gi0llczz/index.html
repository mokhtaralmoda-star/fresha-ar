نبذة

At The Cherry Pit salon, queer alternative stylist Tylore has created a warm, inviting space in the heart of East Vancouver where care and creativity lead every visit. The atmosphere is relaxed and personal, allowing you to feel comfortable and unrushed. Queenie the dog will make your experience as sweet as possible. As a neurodivergent bisexual woman owned studio, this is a place where everyone is valued, supported, and free to be themselves, leaving you feeling seen, confident, and genuinely cared for. Proud member of Strands for Trans.

Haircuts at The Cherry Pit Salon include short cuts, long cuts, and fades, with clients also describing a chemical cut being corrected and dark brown hair transformed to blonde with light pink highlights. The stylist checks in on what clients want, and the salon’s dog is part of the visit.
