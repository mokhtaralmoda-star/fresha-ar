About

Amro Ladies Salon is a luxurious beauty salon with multiple locations throughout the UAE. Conveniently situated in Dubai, Al Ain, and Abu Dhabi, Amro Ladies Salon is the perfect spot to relax and treat yourself. We offer a wide range of high-end hairstyles and beauty treatments, including makeup, waxing, manicures, pedicures, and facials. Merging our years of skill and experience with top-quality products, our goal is to provide you with a service that is second to none.
