نبذة

We provide treatment-based massage services focused on relieving muscular pain, reducing tension, and improving movement. Our clinic supports clients experiencing physical discomfort caused by work, posture, and daily activities. Our therapists use targeted techniques to address specific areas such as neck, shoulders, and lower back. Treatments are tailored to each individual to assist with muscle tightness, restricted movement, and recovery from physical strain. We specialise in remedial and therapeutic massage, with HICAPS available for eligible health fund claims.
