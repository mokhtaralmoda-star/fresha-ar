About

Located in the heart of Riyadh, Maison Hanadi is a luxury beauty destination specializing in professional lash extensions and advanced lash artistry, where precision, expertise, and attention to detail create beautifully customized results. Beyond our signature lash services, we offer an exclusive selection of beauty treatments, including Russian manicures, premium nail enhancements, professional hair styling, and relaxing massage treatments. At Maison Hanadi, we believe that true beauty lies in the details. Our commitment to exceptional quality, personalized care, and refined elegance ensures that every visit is a luxurious experience. Maison Hanadi — Where Every Detail Defines Beauty. 🤍
