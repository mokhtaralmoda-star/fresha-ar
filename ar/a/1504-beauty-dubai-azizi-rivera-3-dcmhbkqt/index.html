About

At 1504 Beauty, we believe every appointment should be an experience of luxury, relaxation, and exceptional care. Our elegant salon has been thoughtfully designed to provide a modern, welcoming space where you can unwind while enjoying personalised treatments tailored to your individual style and beauty goals. From the moment you arrive, our experienced team is dedicated to making you feel comfortable, valued, and confident in every aspect of your visit. We take pride in combining expert techniques with meticulous attention to detail, delivering beautiful results that enhance your natural beauty while reflecting your personal style. Every treatment is carried out with precision, quality, and genuine care, ensuring you leave feeling refreshed, radiant, and your absolute best. Whether you're maintaining your signature look or treating yourself to a well-deserved moment of self-care, our goal is to create an experience you'll love and look forward to every time you visit.
