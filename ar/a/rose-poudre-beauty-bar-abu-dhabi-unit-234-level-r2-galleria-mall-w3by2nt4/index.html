نبذة

Rose Poudre Beauty Bar in Abu Dhabi is a space where girls can get their hair and nails done, The two main players in any girl's feel-good moments. The Galleria in Al Maryah Island was the perfect location to start, since it houses the high scale brands and caters to busy working ladies as well as the fashion forward ones. Rose Poudre is designed in an open plan space where hair wash area, hair styling and Nail section all meet together in a friendly atomshphere. Coffee & tea is served complimentary while you get your favorite service done.
