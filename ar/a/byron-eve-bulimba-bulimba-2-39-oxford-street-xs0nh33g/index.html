نبذة

Byron Eve is a boutique beauty salon, where the focus is on rejuvenation, skin vitality and nurturing the natural effortless beauty of every client. Our team specialises in facial treatments such as hydrating facials, brow sculpting, brow lamination and lash extensions. We also have a cosmetic nurse on site for all your cosmetic treatments and IV infusion therapy needs. You can find our studio at the end of Oxford street, with parking directly out the front. We cannot wait to welcome you in.
