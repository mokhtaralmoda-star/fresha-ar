نبذة

We began our Grace Threading journey in 2009, in the beginning, we were offering threading within our hair salon to compliment hair services. Client demand for expert brow artists has allowed us to expand from one hair salon located at Waterford West that we still operate today, to ten boutique brow threading bars and beauty salons in and around Brisbane, Ipswich, Gold Coast Queensland, Australia. Our mission is to provide a unique environment where women can enjoy a full range of salon services and treatments with high-quality care and total relaxation.
