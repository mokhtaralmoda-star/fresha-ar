نبذة

Elpis Beauty Atelier offers a serene escape where beauty meets relaxation, specializing in a variety of hair and nail services. With a focus on women, the salon provides expert trims, stunning BIAB manicures, and pedicures, all in a pet-friendly and kid-friendly environment. Conveniently located near public transport and with ample parking, it's the perfect spot for a rejuvenating experience.
