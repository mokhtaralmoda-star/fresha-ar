نبذة

EnVie Mobile Massage is a premier provider of health and wellness services exclusively for Women and Seniors in Metro Vancouver. We understand that life can be overwhelming, and sometimes finding time for self-care is challenging. That's why we bring our services to you, wherever you may be - your home, office, or hotel. We provide a safe, convenient, and results-driven experience while treating you with the compassionate care you deserve. We want you to know that we have your back and can't wait to meet you! Our specialized therapies focus on the unique health journeys of women and seniors, addressing chronic pain, mobility restrictions, arthritis, and stress with gentle, tailored techniques. We bring professional, safe care directly to you. Our base service includes Richmond, Central, and South Vancouver. Delta and North/Central Surrey incur a $25 travel fee, while South Surrey incurs a $45 fee. All other areas outside these zones incur an $80 travel fee. Clients must provide a secure parking space upon arrival; any parking meters or fees will be added directly to your final bill. Let us handle all the details while you relax.
