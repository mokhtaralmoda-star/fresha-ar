نبذة

Noir Gents Salon & Spa is a men's barbershop and spa in Zinj, next to Nasser Pharmacy. Our barbers take their time over every cut and shave, and our private spa rooms bring the Moroccan hammam and a proper massage under the same roof. Whether you have thirty minutes or a whole afternoon, you leave sharper and more rested than you arrived. Signature packages pair the chair and the spa in one visit. Open daily from 10 AM to 10 PM. Spa services are not available on Sundays.
