About

Tonic the Method was born as a high-tech wellness protocol in Los Angeles, California. It quickly became a go-to destination for rest, recovery & rejuvenation in a famously busy city. As we continue to grow and reach new communities around the world, this philosophy of tailored support and patient process remains the core of all that we do.
