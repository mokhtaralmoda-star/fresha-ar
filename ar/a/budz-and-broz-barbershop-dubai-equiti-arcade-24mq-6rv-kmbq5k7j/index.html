About

Founded by Reggie Berja, a Master Barber, Budz and Broz is built on years of expertise and dedication to the craft. Reggie began his career as a stylist in 2003 and moved to Dubai in 2008, where he has worked as a professional barber ever since. Through his experience with various companies, he recognized a common issue -a lack of quality and consistency. Determined to change that, he established Budz and Broz, ensuring every client receives the excellence they deserve.
