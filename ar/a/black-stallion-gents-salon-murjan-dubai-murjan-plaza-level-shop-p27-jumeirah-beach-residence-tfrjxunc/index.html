About

Nestled in the upscale Jumeirah Beach Residence, Black Stallion Gents Salon reigns as a sanctuary for the modern gentleman. Since 2010, this renowned establishment has provided exceptional grooming experiences through its team of skilled barbers and beauticians. Whether a haircut and beard trim, rejuvenating with a keratin or hair treatment, or opting for hand and foot care, facials, and precise hair removal, meticulous attention to detail transforms every visit into a transformative journey of self-care and sophisticated style.
