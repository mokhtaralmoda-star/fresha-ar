About

TwoNineSix Studio is a photography studio in Al Ain dedicated to capturing life’s meaningful moments through light, emotion, and timeless storytelling. We believe that every photograph should preserve more than an image—it should reflect a feeling, a connection, and a memory that can be cherished for years to come. With a thoughtful and artistic approach, we create portraits and visual stories that celebrate people, milestones, and the moments that matter most. Whether documenting a personal achievement, a family memory, or a special occasion, our goal is to create authentic imagery that feels natural, elegant, and enduring. At TwoNineSix Studio, we provide a welcoming and comfortable experience, allowing every client to feel at ease in front of the camera while we transform meaningful moments into lasting memories.
