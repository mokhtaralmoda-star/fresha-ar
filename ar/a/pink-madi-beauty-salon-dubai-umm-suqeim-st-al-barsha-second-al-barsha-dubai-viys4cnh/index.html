About

PINK MADI BEAUTY SALON Where European Elegance Meets Moroccan Luxury ✨ Inspired by the world’s finest luxury destinations, Pink Madi is the creation of a Spanish-Moroccan founder with a passion for exceptional beauty, elegance and wellness. Discover our signature Moroccan Hammam, Japanese Head Spa, luxurious massages, expert hair styling, premium hair treatments, extensions, manicures, pedicures, Gelish, nail extensions, lashes, brows and rejuvenating facials. We believe true luxury lies in every detail. That’s why we work with prestigious international brands, including Thalgo, Nashi, Kerasilk, K18, OPI, ORLY and Essie, bringing premium professional products to every beauty experience. Our Exclusive Private VIP Beauty Room offers the ultimate time-saving luxury, with up to three beauty specialists attending to you simultaneously for a seamless, personalised experience.
