About

LAM Nails & Spa is where beauty meets joy! Inspired by my two little girls, LAM is a cozy, feel-good space in Riyadh for nails, hair, and everyday pampering. We keep things simple, fresh, and fun — from clean BIAB to colorful nail art and easy hair touch-ups. Whether you’re a busy mom, a student on the go, or just need some “me time,” you’re always part of the LAM family. Come as you are — leave feeling better.
