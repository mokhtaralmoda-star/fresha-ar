نبذة

Glow Nails Studio is a private, home-based nail studio specializing in premium Ukrainian (Russian) dry manicure techniques, builder gel strengthening, and beautiful natural-looking nail enhancements This technique is different from a traditional manicure, focusing on detailed precise natural nail preparation, and a clean, elegant, long - lasting finish. Each appointment is personalized and takes place in a clean,relaxing environment ,where every client receives one-on-one attention. I use professional, fully sterilized tools and carefully selected, high-quality professional products to ensure a safe, comfortable, and long- lasting result Exceptional results take time. Appointments may take longer than a traditional nail service, as every visit is performed with patience, precision and attention to every detail.
