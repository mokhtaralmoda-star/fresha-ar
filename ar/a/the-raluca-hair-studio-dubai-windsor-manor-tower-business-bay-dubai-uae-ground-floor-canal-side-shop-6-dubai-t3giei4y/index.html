About

THE RALUCA Hair Studio is a women-only hair studio in Business Bay, Dubai, created for women who want professional hair services in a calm, elegant and transparent environment. Our experienced female hairstylists focus on healthy hair, premium professional products and honest advice before every service. We offer haircuts, blowdry, gloss, root color, full color, balayage, highlights, K18 repair treatments and polished styling. We believe beautiful hair starts with trust. That is why we explain the options clearly, confirm everything before we start and never pressure our clients.
