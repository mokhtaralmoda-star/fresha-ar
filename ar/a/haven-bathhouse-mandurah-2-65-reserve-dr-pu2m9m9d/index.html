نبذة

A sanctuary for slowing down in the heart of Mandurah. Haven is a premium, shared all-gender bathhouse offering a 90-minute experience centred around heat, water and stillness. Move between our hot and warm magnesium pools, cold immersion, steam room and Finnish saunas, taking your time to create your own ritual. Designed with natural textures, warm tones and greenery, Haven offers a calm space to step away from the everyday and simply be. 18+ only · Swimwear required · Limited guest numbers
