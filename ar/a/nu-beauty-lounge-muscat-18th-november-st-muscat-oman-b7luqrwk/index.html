About

NÜ Beauty Lounge is a luxury beauty destination dedicated to exceptional technique, refined care and attention to every detail. Our highly skilled Russian Nail Masters specialize in precision Russian manicures, SMART pedicures, nail extensions and beautifully detailed finishes. Complemented by professional hair services and indulgent spa rituals featuring premium VOESH New York products, every NÜ experience is thoughtfully curated to leave you feeling polished, pampered and beautifully renewed.
