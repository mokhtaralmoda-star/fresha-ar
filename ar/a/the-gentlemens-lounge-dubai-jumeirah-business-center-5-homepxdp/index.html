About

Situated in Cluster "W" of Jumeirah lake towers, The Gentlemen's Lounge is one of the best barbershops in the area, offering a range of grooming services from their base. From cuts and shaves to facials, massages and hair removal, all needs are catered for. The team of professional groomers have a wealth of experience, and the salon remains open until 10pm from Saturday to Thursday.
