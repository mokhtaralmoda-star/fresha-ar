نبذة

Al Hayat Ladies Salon and Spa is a ladies beauty salon in Al Muntazah, Doha, offering hair styling, haircuts, hair coloring, highlights and balayage, keratin and protein treatments, and hair spa services. We also provide manicures, pedicures, nail extensions, lash services, waxing, facials, makeup and relaxing spa treatments. Located on C Ring Road, we welcome clients from Al Muntazah and nearby Doha areas, including Al Sadd and Bin Mahmoud. Our team focuses on attentive service, professional beauty care and a comfortable, welcoming salon experience tailored to your style and needs.
