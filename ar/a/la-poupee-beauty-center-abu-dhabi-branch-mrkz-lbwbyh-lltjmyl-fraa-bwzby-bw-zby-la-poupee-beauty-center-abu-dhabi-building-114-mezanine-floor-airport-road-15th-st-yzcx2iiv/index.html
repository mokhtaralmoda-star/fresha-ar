نبذة

Since 1999, La Poupee Beauty Center has been a trusted women-only beauty destination in Abu Dhabi. We offer a complete beauty and wellness experience under one roof — professional hair services, hair colour and treatments, nail care, facials, massage and body treatments, waxing and hair removal, lashes, brows, and makeup. With over 25 years of expertise, our team of trained beauty professionals combines personalized care with warm hospitality to make every visit feel special. Whether you are looking for a quick nail appointment or a full day of pampering, La Poupee is your home for beauty in Abu Dhabi. Women-only salon | Established 1999 | Airport Road, Abu Dhabi
