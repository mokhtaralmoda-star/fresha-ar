About

Promise Hair Spa offers the world’s best hair treatments, including exclusive Japanese Hair Spa and innovative techniques. Founded by three French experts, we’ve created our own products, protocols, and a relaxing, luxurious experience. From personalized hair treatment to viral hair spa, to amazing balayage and color services, we specialize in bringing strength, beauty shine, and health to your hair and scalp. Discover our unique approach to beauty and leave feeling confident, and radiant. Experience excellence in every detail.
