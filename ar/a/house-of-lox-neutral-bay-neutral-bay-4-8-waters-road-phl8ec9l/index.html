نبذة

A boutique hair salon on Sydney's Lower North Shore, local to the Cremorne, Mosman, Neutral Bay, North Sydney and Northern Beaches areas. House of Lox are not ordinary hairdressers – our staff are the resident experts in hair extensions and modern colour techniques, and routinely perform miraculous transformations for our loyal clients seeking a new look or a boost in confidence. We keep a full range of tape hair extensions in stock for range of seamless tapes, bonds and weft extensions and custom order Beauty Works hair extensions.
