نبذة

Mane Haven is a boutique hair studio in the heart of Mornington, built on one belief: great hair should feel as good as it looks. We're known for precision haircutting and lived-in, natural-looking colour, work that enhances who you are rather than changing it. Every appointment starts with a proper consultation, because the best results come from understanding your hair, your routine and how you actually want to feel walking out the door. Our team is education-led and always learning. From emerging stylists to our Director, every service is shaped by technique, care and a genuine love of the craft. We welcome every hair type and texture, and we're here between appointments too, with honest advice and a home routine to protect your results. Beyond the salon floor, our bridal team travels across the Mornington Peninsula for your wedding day. Premium without the pretence. Polished without the intimidation. Open six days, 8am 'til late. Come as you are.
