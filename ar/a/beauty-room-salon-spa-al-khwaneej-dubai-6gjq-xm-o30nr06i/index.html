About

Established in 2014 by the dynamic Emirati mother-daughter duo of Ayesha Ali and Samira Abdulrahman, Beauty Room -Salon & SPA is a cherished family-run venture based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Since its inception, it has proliferated into four premier venues spread across Dubai. We've earned a sizeable, loyal clientele, a testament to our unwavering commitment and diligent work. Our salon boasts a relaxing ambiance and a formidable team of over 85 innovative professionals, dedicated to providing unparalleled quality services.
