نبذة

AROKA is a middle Indo-Aryan liturgical language, which means "No diseases." There is a saying in Thai, "No disease is a great fortune," signifying the absence of diseases in both the body and the mind. "Health" and "Life" are interdependent; one cannot fully function without the other. Therefore, to live is to be healthy, and to be healthy is to have a full life. At AROKA Therapeutic Centre, we are proud to offer you a sanctuary for a well-maintained body, good health, and a sound mind.
