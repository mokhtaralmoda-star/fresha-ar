About

SOAK is a chic and sophisticated sanctuary where beauty meets relaxation. Located in Jumeirah 1 in the heart of Dubai, our salon offers a wide range of premium services, including expert haircare, flawless nail and foot treatments, as well as a full range of lash and brow options. Designed with modern elegance, SOAK provides a welcoming space for women to unwind and prioritize self-care.
