نبذة

Flirty Gertie Beauty Bar is a boutique beauty salon in South Fremantle. For over 14 years the team at FG have been pampering locals with a menu that includes a variety of facials, peels and skin care, from problematic teen skin to anti ageing regimes. Specialising in brows and waxing (including all men's waxing), feather touch brows, lash lifts, brow lamination, spray tans, massage, special occasion make up. They are stockists of the cutting-edge cosmecceutial range Germaine de Capuccini (#1 in Spain and Italy) and Jonte and Jordyn the Label-a new clothing label crafted for women .
