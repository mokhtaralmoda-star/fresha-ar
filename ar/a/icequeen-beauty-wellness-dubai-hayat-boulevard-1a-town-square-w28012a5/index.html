About

Established in 2021, ICEQUEEN Beauty Salon is your neighborhood beauty destination on Hayat Boulevard, proudly serving Town Square and surrounding communities. From nail and hair care to facials, cosmetology, waxing and threading, we bring all your beauty essentials together under one roof. Our multicultural, predominantly Russian-speaking team combines professional expertise with a personal touch, making every visit comfortable, welcoming and all about you.
