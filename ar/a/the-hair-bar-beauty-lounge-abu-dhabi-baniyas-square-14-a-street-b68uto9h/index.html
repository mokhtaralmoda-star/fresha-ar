About

Located in Baniyas, The Hair Bar Salon offers a wide range of trendy haircuts, styling, makeup, hair coloring, waxing, spray tanning, and facials. Our team of highly skilled and experienced stylists is dedicated to delivering top-notch beauty services tailored to your needs. Whether you are looking for a fresh haircut, vibrant hair color, flawless makeup, or rejuvenating skincare, The Hair Bar Salon ensures a luxurious and personalized experience. Visit us for exceptional service and stylish results that leave you feeling confident and refreshed.
