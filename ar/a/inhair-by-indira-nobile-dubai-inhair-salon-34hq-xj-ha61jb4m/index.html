About

InHair is a luxury hair salon in the heart of Dubai Marina, founded by Indira Nobile — a hair artist with over 14 years of experience and a background spanning Italy and London. Specialising in blondes, balayage, and advanced colour techniques, InHair is the destination for clients who want beautiful results without compromising the health of their hair. Every service is personalised, every detail considered. Whether you're looking for a full colour transformation, a precision cut, or a relaxing treatment, you'll leave feeling like the best version of yourself — in a calm, welcoming space where you're always in expert hands.
