About

Razor Barber Shop based in the green heart of Dubai, Al Barari. It provides the most indulgent services for men to look relaxed while they are getting groomed to be in style. Razor is a place where everyone can proudly let their individuality shine especially with the styles our experienced and talented staff provides to the clients. We offer a full menu of high-end, premium service specializing in men’s hair cuts, colors, facials, and traditional shave.
