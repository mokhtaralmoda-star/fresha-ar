نبذة

CRYO Infinity Jeddah is a premium wellness and performance destination in Jeddah, offering advanced experiences designed to support relaxation, rejuvenation, recovery, and an active lifestyle. Our services include Whole Body Cryotherapy, Red Light, Body Lymphatic Compression, HALSA Oxygen, and Localized Cryotherapy. Guests enjoy a modern, private, and carefully designed environment with professional guidance throughout their visit. Located in Abhur Al Janubia, we welcome both women and men from Saturday to Thursday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Friday is our weekly day off. Advance booking is recommended.
