نبذة

Bikini Moon is a boutique salon which stands out for its dedication to providing exceptional hair removal and beauty services in a welcoming environment. With over 15 years of expertise, we specialise in Brazilian waxing while utilising organic and vegan products to ensure a gentle experience for all clients. Emphasising inclusivity, Bikini Moon is a space where everyone can embrace their confidence and feel at ease during their visit. Also at Bikini Moon is Luxe Rituals by Esmeralda. An experienced Lash Artist, Esmeralda specialises in creating beautiful, customised lash sets designed specifically for you. Rather than following a one-size-fits-all approach, every set is tailored to complement your eye shape, facial features and personal style. Whether you prefer soft and natural, fluffy and noticeable, or full glam, Esmeralda will create a set that feels perfectly you.
