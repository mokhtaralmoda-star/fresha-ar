About

Discover The 971 Lounge Gents Salon, a traditional barbershop located in Al Nahda’s AR1 Tower. We offer a wide range of grooming services for men and children, from hair coloring and highlights to beard shaving, trimming, and stylish blow-dry finishes. Enjoy a relaxing manicure and pedicure in our massage chairs, or indulge in customized hair treatments and refreshing facials. Our expert team is dedicated to ensuring you leave feeling polished and revitalized. Visit us today for the ultimate grooming experience!
