About

Elitestyle Polyclinic offers a refined medspa experience, specializing in advanced laser hair removal techniques, including Candela and Diode lasers, ensuring optimal results for every skin type. Their innovative services also feature tattoo removal, EMSculpt, and Cryolipolysis, designed to enhance your beauty and well-being. With a commitment to adult clients, the clinic provides a serene and sophisticated atmosphere, complete with convenient parking for an effortless visit.
