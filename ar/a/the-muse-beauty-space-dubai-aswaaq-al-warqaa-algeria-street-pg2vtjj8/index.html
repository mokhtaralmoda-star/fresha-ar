نبذة

The Muse Beauty Space Salon, located in Aswaaq Al Warqa Mall, Dubai, offers high-quality services for ladies, including facials, hair treatments, nail services, Moroccan hammam, massages, waxing, henna, and makeup. The salon uses premium brands to ensure professional, high-quality hair and beauty treatments. Clients have praised the salon for its excellent service, professional staff, and relaxing atmosphere. The salon is open daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
