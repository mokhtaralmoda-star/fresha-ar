About

At Amro Ladies Salon - Chedi Branch, beauty meets comfort in a serene environment where every detail is designed for your relaxation. Offering a variety of services including Gelish polish, nail fixes, and deep cleaning, this salon caters to all your beauty needs while prioritizing eco-friendliness. With amenities such as parking and a welcoming atmosphere for both pets and children, it’s the perfect destination for a rejuvenating experience.
