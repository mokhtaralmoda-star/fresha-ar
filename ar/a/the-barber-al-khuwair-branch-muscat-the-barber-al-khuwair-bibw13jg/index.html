About

The Barber is simply a Classic Men's barber shop offering similar high class services as in the USA and Europe. Whether you have curly hair, kinky hair, straight hair, black, brown, blonde or white hair, we handle it all. Business, business casual, bangs, hip style, no matter what style you want, if it exists in the market, we can get you the look you want and if it doesn't exist we will exercise patience to understand what you want and get you the look you want. Every person is different, we will help you be you! You can expect the same class service in all our locations.
