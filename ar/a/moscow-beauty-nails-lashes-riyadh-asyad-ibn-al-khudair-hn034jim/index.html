About

Moscow Beauty Salon, located in Jeddah, is a premium beauty destination specializing in expert nail services and luxurious lash extensions. Offering a range of treatments from precision manicures and pedicures to elegant, long-lasting lash extensions, the salon provides personalized care in a relaxing, chic environment. Whether you're looking for a flawless set of nails or dramatic lashes, Moscow Beauty Salon combines top-tier products with skilled professionals to ensure you leave feeling pampered and confident.
