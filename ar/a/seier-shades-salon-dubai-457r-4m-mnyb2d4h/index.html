About

Shades Salon is a premier beauty destination for women of all hair types and textures. It is conveniently located in Al Barsha 1, Dubai, near the Mall of the Emirates and Sharaf DG metro station. We specialize in hair treatments, extensions, braids, relaxers, keratin treatments, manicures, pedicures, threading, and waxing. As the best salon in Dubai for natural and curly hair, our expert team is dedicated to enhancing your beauty and ensuring you leave feeling fabulous. Experience personalized service and top-quality care at Shades Salon your go-to for all your beauty needs!
