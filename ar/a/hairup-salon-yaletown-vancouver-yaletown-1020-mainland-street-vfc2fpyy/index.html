نبذة

Discover HairUp Salon, a premium hair destination in the heart of Yaletown, Vancouver. Our team includes European stylists with 5 to 24 years of professional experience, combining advanced techniques with personalized care for every client. We offer precision Haircuts, Highlights, Balayage, Colour Correction, Bellami Hair Extensions, Keratin Smoothing, Hair Botox, Deep-Conditioning Treatments, Blowouts and Styling. We also provide technology-assisted hair and scalp analysis to better understand your hair’s condition and recommend suitable salon services and home care. Founded by Tetiana Ikhno, HairUp is committed to exceptional results while protecting the health, strength, and beauty of your hair. We use carefully selected, high-quality professional materials from trusted brands, including Napura Italy, MTJ Italy, Oribe, R+Co, and Janeke. Whether you want a subtle refresh, complete transformation, or restorative care, our experienced team will create a look tailored to you. Experience European expertise, modern technology, and premium hair care at HairUp Salon.
