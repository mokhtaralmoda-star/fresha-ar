About

Her Place is an exclusive beauty home studio located in the heart of Jumeirah Village Circle (JVC), in the building The Vybe – by Metrical Development. Our studio offers high-quality beauty treatments in a stylish, private, and relaxing atmosphere. The business is run by a German beauty expert with many years of experience in the beauty industry. We work exclusively with high-quality products of German high quality, ensuring professional, safe, and first-class results for our clients. As a private home studio, we place special emphasis on hygiene, quality, precision, and personal care. Our goal is to provide every client with an exclusive beauty experience where she can fully relax and feel comfortable. ✨ Her Place is more than just a beauty studio – it is a small oasis where women can unwind and enjoy professional beauty treatments in a calm and luxurious environment.
