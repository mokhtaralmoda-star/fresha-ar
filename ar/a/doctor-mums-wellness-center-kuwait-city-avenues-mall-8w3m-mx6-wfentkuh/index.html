About

At Doctor Mum's Wellness Center, the focus is on nurturing both mothers and their little ones in a serene environment. Offering unique services like the Lactation Boost Massage and a luxury Mama & Baby spa experience, this spa provides a refreshing escape for families. With amenities such as a swimming pool and sauna, every visit promises relaxation and rejuvenation, all while upholding a commitment to organic and vegan products.
