About

NjM Barbershop is a hidden treasure among Riyadh's barbershops. Our hair salon provides a variety of treatments to fulfill all of your grooming needs. We have you covered with expert hair style and accurate beard cutting, as well as colorful hair coloring and relaxing oil massages. Enter our modern and pleasant location, where our expert barbers will provide you a personalized service.
