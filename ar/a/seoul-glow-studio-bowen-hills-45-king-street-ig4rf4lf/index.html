نبذة

Seoul Glow Studio is a Korean hair and beauty salon on King Street, Bowen Hills, bringing the precision and soft, natural styling of Seoul's top salons to Brisbane. We specialise in Korean perms (digital perm, setting perm, root perm, fringe perm and sculpt curl perm), magic straightening, hair colour, balayage and Korean-style cuts for women and men. Our nail and lash studio offers Korean nail art, gel, eyelash extensions and lash lifts. Our team is led by Hair Director Kai, with senior designers Julie and Siah and our nail and lash specialist. Every service starts with a consultation so we can design a look that suits your hair, face shape and lifestyle. Open 7 days: Mon–Fri 10am–7pm, Sat–Sun 9am–6pm. 2 hours free parking at King Street Parking. We speak English, Korean and Mandarin – 欢迎中文咨询. *Please note : A 10% surcharge applies on Sundays and Public holidays.
