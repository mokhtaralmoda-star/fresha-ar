About

Heavenston Beauty Salon is known for its cozy neighborhood feel, quiet atmosphere, and attentive customer care. A welcoming escape from the day, we offer nails, facial services, Moroccan baths, waxing, and specialized hair treatments, including nourishing organic masks. With homemade manicure and pedicure scrubs and lotions made from natural ingredients, every visit feels personal, relaxing, and worth returning to.
