About

Marcel Salon is a full-service hair and beauty salon offering contemporary services in a relaxed and welcoming environment. We understand how important it is to look your best! Our certified hairstylists have the skills and experience to help you achieve the perfect look, from a haircut and style to fully customized color with highlights, as well as amazing skin and nails. We pride ourselves on providing access to top-of-the-line beauty and hair products. Enjoy the privacy of your own room if you wish, at no extra charge.
