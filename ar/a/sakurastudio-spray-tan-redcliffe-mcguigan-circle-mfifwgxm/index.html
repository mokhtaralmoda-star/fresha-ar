نبذة

Hi! My name is Carly and I created SakuraStudio as a friendly, welcoming, and inclusive spray tan salon which I’ve recently moved from a commercial space into my home. My mission is to ensure you feel comfortable throughout your tanning experience, especially if you're new to it or feel a little anxious. As I primarily offer bridal spray tans, I specialise in customising your tan to suit your skin tone, dress and jewellery, and making sure that you have the most even, long lasting tan possible! I use only top-quality products and will always provide you with thorough guidance on how to prep for your tan and care for it afterwards. Pre-tan prep coat and post-tanning top coats are available to add on, with drying spray AND setting powder included with all tans for any crease-prone and sweat-prone areas - so you will always leave feeling fresh, dry, and never sticky!
