About

Craftsmanship meets modernity at BLADE, where every precise cut is treated to an exceptional gentlemen care experience. Specializing in everything from precise hair lining to soothing Moroccan baths, this Omani-owned barbershop is dedicated to using only the best in organic products. With amenities like showers and lockers, as well as a kid-friendly and wheelchair-accessible environment, BLADE ensures a welcoming space for all.
