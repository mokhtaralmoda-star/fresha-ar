About

Established in the year 2023, Innbeauty Space is popular salon located in the heart of Dubai Jumeirah 1, just 10 minutes away from Burj Khalifa. Founded by Celebrity Make up Artist Julia Malygina. Salon offers a wide range of services starting from make up, styling to hair colors, eyelash extensions to nails services. In addition to that the team of professionals can create complimentary content for the client. Innbeauty Space chosen by celebrities for the high level of quality and the innovative approach.
