About

At Bellacures Nails & Lashes Salon, clients are treated to an exquisite range of nail services, from gel manicures and pedicures to meticulous old gel polish removal. This women-only, environmentally friendly salon prides itself on creating a welcoming space, with a focus on quality and attention to detail. With amenities catering to families and a skilled staff fluent in English and Russian, your visit promises to be both enjoyable and relaxing.
