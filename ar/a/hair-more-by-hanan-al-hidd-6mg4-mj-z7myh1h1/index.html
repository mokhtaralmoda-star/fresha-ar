About

Hair & More by Hanan is a ladies-only salon in Hidd, specialising in beautiful hair colour and cutting. Hanan and Mona are both trained at Dessange. We also offer nails, brows, lashes and waxing in a calm, private space with convenient parking. Proudly serving Hidd, East Hidd, Arad and Busaiteen.
