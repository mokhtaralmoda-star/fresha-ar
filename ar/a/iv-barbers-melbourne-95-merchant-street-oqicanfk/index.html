نبذة

Since 2017, IV Barbers has redefined men’s grooming in Docklands, Melbourne—blending craftsmanship, precision and indulgence. We specialise in precision men’s haircuts, scissor cutting for short, mid-length and longer hair, signature fades, beard trims and sculpting, and traditional hot towel shaves. Located at 95 Merchant Street, Docklands, our barbershop offers a calm, elevated retreat from the everyday. Every haircut is delivered with care and refined technique, including a hair wash, luxurious hot towel, precision neck shave and personalised styling guidance to suit your individual look.
