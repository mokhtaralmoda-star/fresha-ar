In a charming corner of Seville Grove, Brow & Glow Studio elevates the art of beauty with a focus on eyebrows and lashes. With a commitment to inclusivity, Brow & Glow Studio creates a welcoming space for all, ensuring every client leaves feeling confident and radiant.

Brow & Glow Studio offers a more personal appointment than a quick in-and-out: the brow artist takes time with unruly brows and shapes them to suit the face. Choose from laminated brows, Brow Wax & Tint, or a Lash Lift & Tint; spray tans are finished with drying powder.
