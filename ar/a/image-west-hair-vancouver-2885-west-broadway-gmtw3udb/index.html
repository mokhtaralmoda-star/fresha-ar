Image West is your one stop shop for all things curl! Whether you’re looking for a specialized curly cut, or professional products, our salon and store is here for you, along with our highly knowledgeable team. We carry over 30 different brands in store for all your hair care needs! With over 25 years in business, Image West has become a staple in the Kitsilano community.
