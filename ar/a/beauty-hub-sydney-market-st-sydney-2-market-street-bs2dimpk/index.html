نبذة

Beauty Hub Sydney is a premium skin and aesthetics clinic in the heart of the CBD, at 2 Market Street. We specialise in advanced skin, lifting and beauty treatments, delivered with medical-grade technology and a clinical approach. Every journey starts with a consultation and Observ 520x skin analysis, so your plan is built around what's happening beneath the surface. Our treatments include Liftera HIFU, RF and RF needling, IPL, skin needling, Plexr, medical-grade facials and body contouring, alongside expert brows, lashes and permanent makeup. Our in-clinic doctors, Dr Denise Huang and Dr Jessie Ning from Cosmedical Doctors, offer cosmetic doctor consultations on alternate Tuesdays and Saturdays. Whether you're after firmer skin, a clearer complexion or perfectly defined brows, every treatment is tailored to help you look refreshed, confident and naturally radiant.
