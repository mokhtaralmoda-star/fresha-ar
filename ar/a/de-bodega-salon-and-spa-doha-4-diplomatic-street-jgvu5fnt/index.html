About

At De Bodega Salon and Spa, we believe beauty is an experience that should leave you feeling relaxed, confident, and truly cared for. Since opening in August 2024, we have created a luxurious and welcoming space where every client can enjoy personalised attention and a moment dedicated to themselves. Our passionate team is committed to providing exceptional care through professional expertise, quality products, and a thoughtful approach tailored to each individual. Every visit is designed to offer comfort, relaxation, and beautiful results in an environment where you can unwind and feel your best. Whether you're looking for a refreshing self-care experience or a chance to enhance your natural beauty, we strive to make every appointment memorable and leave you feeling rejuvenated, confident, and valued.
