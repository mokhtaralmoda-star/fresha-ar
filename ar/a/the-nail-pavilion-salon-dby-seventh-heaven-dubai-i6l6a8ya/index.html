About

Step into a relaxed environment where simplicity shines and luxury reigns. Enjoy a truly distinctive experience where all your needs are met. The Nail Pavilion is a beauty salon offering the best state of the art beauty services, and the latest products selected from around the world. The first location opened in 2014 by owner Aamna Mani, The Nail Pavilion is based in Cluster Y, JLT and Al Barari, and has an extensive treatment list, including manicures, pedicures, waxing, eye care, massages, and hairstyling. The Nail Pavilion also offers at-home services.
