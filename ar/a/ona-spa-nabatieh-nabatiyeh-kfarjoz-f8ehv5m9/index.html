About

Ona Spa redefines self-care with its luxurious offerings designed to elevate your beauty experience in Nabatiyeh. Specializing in organic treatments, from rejuvenating Cocoon Hydration masks to intricate nail designs, this woman-owned beauty haven prioritizes environmentally friendly practices. With amenities like convenient parking and showers, visitors can indulge in a serene, adults-only atmosphere that promotes relaxation and rejuvenation.
