About

Irene Toma Women’s Salon is a luxury hair salon in Jumeirah, Dubai, inspired by French elegance and a refined Parisian touch. Designed exclusively for women, we offer a calm, high-end experience with services including balayage, blond, colour, and professional hairstyling tailored to enhance natural beauty. We also provide expert makeup services by Sam. Enjoy premium Italian coffee as you relax in a truly refined and indulgent setting. ✨
