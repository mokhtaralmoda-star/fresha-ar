About

Our second branch, inaugurated over two years ago, is nestled in the heart of Marina at the Intercontinental Hotel. Our team consists of highly trained European stylists who stay ahead of current UK and European hair trends. At Chill Salon, we relish the creative challenge of high-fashion colouring, including ombre and highlighting techniques. We believe that meticulous consultations and precise execution truly set Chill Salon apart.
