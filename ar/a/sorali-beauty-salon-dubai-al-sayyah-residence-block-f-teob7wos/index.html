About

Sorali Beauty Salon is a professional beauty salon in Arjan, Dubai, offering hair, nails, lashes, skincare and beauty treatments for women and children. Our services include haircuts and styling, hair colouring, hair treatments, children’s hair services, facials, waxing, threading, manicures, pedicures and lash treatments. Conveniently located in Arjan, we welcome clients from Arjan and surrounding Dubai communities looking for a trusted hair salon, nail salon and beauty salon. Whether you’re visiting for a fresh colour, relaxing facial, flawless nails or regular beauty maintenance, the Sorali team provides friendly, personalised service in a comfortable and welcoming salon environment.
