About

ARU SPA & BEAUTY LOUNGE is a women-only beauty and wellness destination in Qurum, Muscat, created for relaxation, self-care and effortless beauty. Enjoy professional hair colouring and styling, Korean Head Spa, relaxing massages, rejuvenating facials, nail care, waxing and other beauty treatments in a calm and welcoming atmosphere. Our goal is to make every visit a moment to slow down, recharge and leave feeling confident, beautiful and cared for.
