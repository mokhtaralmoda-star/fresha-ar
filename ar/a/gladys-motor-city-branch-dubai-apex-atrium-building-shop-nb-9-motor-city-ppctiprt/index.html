About

Founded in 2001, it is an exclusive beauty destination for all your hair, nail, and body treatments. Set in Five different locations across Dubai, at Gladys Beauty Salon. We welcome you into a soothing ambiance where you can unwind, de-stress, and get a fabulous makeover! Gladys Beauty Salon is located in Motor City in the Apex Atrium Building, close to the Dubai Autodrome.
