About

Be Bar is Dubai’s premier hair health and hairstyling salon specializing in a range of blow dry styles, updos, braids, and intensive scalp and hair treatments, including the Brazilian Blowout smoothing treatment. We also offer add-on services including manicure/pedicure, threading, waxing, and express massages. Clients are treated to a luxe wash and soothing mini scalp massage on lie-flat beds in our tranquil shampoo lounge. The menu offers something for everyone, from everyday styles for women. Bespoke services are on offer for bridal parties, photoshoots, and other private events.
