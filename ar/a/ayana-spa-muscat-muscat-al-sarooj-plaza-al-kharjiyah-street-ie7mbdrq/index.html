About

Ayana Spa is Oman’s first independent day spa, which opened in May 2004. Our spa spans 700 square meters of space, imparting positive energy. Upon entering our doors, you will be welcomed by a relaxing ambience of soothing music and the fragrance of pleasant organic aromatic oils that fill your senses. Inspired by nature, we use only products that are safe for the body and the environment. We offer a selection of unique treatments from around the world to rejuvenate and beautify the skin and hair, calm the mind, and harmonise the soul. Our spa's professionally trained therapists aim to exceed.
