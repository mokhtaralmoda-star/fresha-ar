About

The name VIVANT is a French word that means alive. We celebrate life by making every important moment more memorable for you. Our salon's mission is to make sure all women receive the pamper and beauty they deserve most. A woman’s beauty is one of our missions. We offer services from hair, nails, skin, and body treatments to eyelash extensions with the best quality possible. Together with our skilled and well-trained technicians through updated and modern products and technology, we provide a luxurious experience to all our busy moms and hardworking women out there.
