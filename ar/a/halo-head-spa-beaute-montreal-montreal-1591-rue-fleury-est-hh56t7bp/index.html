Halo Head Spa & Beauté — Your Escape into Serenity. Step into a world of relaxation, sensory indulgence, and personalized beauty at Halo Head Spa & Beauté in Montréal. Inspired by Japanese wellness rituals, we bring together the art of relaxation and the beauty of self-care to create experiences that nurture your senses and invite you to slow down. From our immersive Japanese Head Spa rituals and relaxing Dry Head Spa to our indulgent Foot Spa, ASMR & Scratching Massage, personalized facials, back treatments, and unique sensory experiences, every ritual is thoughtfully designed to offer a moment just for you. Whether you're looking to unwind, indulge your senses, or enjoy a complete head-to-toe escape, discover a calming space where every detail is designed to make you feel cared for. Your time. Your ritual. Your moment of serenity. Welcome to Halo.
