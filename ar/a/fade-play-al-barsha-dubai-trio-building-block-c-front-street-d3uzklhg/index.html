نبذة

Fade & Play Al Barsha is a top-rated barbershop near Mall of the Emirates, just 2 minutes away, serving clients from Al Barsha, Al Barsha Heights (Tecom), Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah Lake Towers (JLT), and Umm Suqeim. Our expert barbers specialize in skin fades, precision haircuts, beard sculpting, Afro hair, braids, and dreadlocks. We also offer grooming services including facials, manicure, and pedicure. Known for skilled barbers and consistent quality, Fade & Play provides a premium grooming experience trusted by clients across Dubai.
