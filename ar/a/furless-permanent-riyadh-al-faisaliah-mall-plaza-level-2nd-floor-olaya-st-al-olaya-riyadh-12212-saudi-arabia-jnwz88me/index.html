About

Furless Permanent is the world's largest specialist provider of electrolysis. Unlike laser, electrolysis is permanent hair removal that works on all hair colours, skin tones and body areas. Electrolysis is the only FDA-approved method of permanent hair removal for both men and women. On average, treatment can take between 12 - 18 months, and appointments are required every 15 days. Once your treatment plan is complete, you are permanently 'Furless'. Ready to begin your electrolysis journey today? Book your skin and hair assessment and discovery treatment. Call, WhatsApp or book directly through Fresha today.
