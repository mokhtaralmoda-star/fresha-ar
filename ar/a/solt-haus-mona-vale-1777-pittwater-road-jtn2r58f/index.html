نبذة

Sôlt Haus - pronounced “salt house” - is a wellness studio like no other. We design slow, gentle wellbeing rituals that nurture body, mind, skin and soul. Our therapies are grounded in ancient practices and borrow ingredients from nature. In a world crowded in expense and excess, we find solace in simplicity and choose always to return to the wisdom of the wild. Our studio is located in the heart of Mona Vale on the Northern Beaches of NSW. We look forward to welcoming you! Pop in for a quickie, book a combo of therapies, or become a member and take full advantage.
