نبذة

On Her Nails is a boutique nail salon located on Weston Road in North York, offering a calm, clean, and welcoming space for beautiful nail care. Our goal is to make every client feel comfortable, cared for, and confident from the moment they walk in. Whether you are looking for a simple clean manicure, a relaxing pedicure, a fresh shellac set, or something more stylish and detailed, our team is here to help bring your vision to life. Walk-ins are welcome when available, but appointments are recommended to secure your preferred time.
