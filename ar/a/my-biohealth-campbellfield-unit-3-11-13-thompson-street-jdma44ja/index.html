نبذة

My BioHealth is a nurse-led wellness, recovery and skin clinic in Campbellfield, serving Broadmeadows, Craigieburn, Roxburgh Park and Melbourne’s northern suburbs. Wellness and recovery: mild hyperbaric oxygen therapy at 1.35 ATA, full-body red light therapy, infrared sauna, magnesium float therapy, recovery boots and pelvic floor chair sessions. Skin and aesthetics: PRP and PRF for skin and hair, skin needling, CO2 laser resurfacing, carbon laser facial and laser tattoo removal. Every service is delivered within nursing scope of practice, following individual consultation and screening. Weekly memberships and session packs available. Individual results vary and are not guaranteed.
