About

The word 'La Colmena' is derived from the Spanish word meaning 'hive.' We have designed a space that connects you to the hive of nature, where you are treated with tender love and care. We are not just a place for you to beautify yourself from the outside, but we also help you connect to your inner beauty. Our salon is designed to bring you closer to nature and treat you with the freshness and natural elements it has to offer. La Colmena is a beauty hive created to treat you with tender love and care.
