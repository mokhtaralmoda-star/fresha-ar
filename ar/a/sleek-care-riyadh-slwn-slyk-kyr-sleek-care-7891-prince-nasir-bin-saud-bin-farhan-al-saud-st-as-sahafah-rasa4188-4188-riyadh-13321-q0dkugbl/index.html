About

Sleek Care stands as a beacon of style and sophistication in Riyadh, offering an array of hair services that cater to every individual's needs. From precision haircuts and vibrant hair color to luxurious blow-dry treatments and expertly crafted balayage, the salon ensures each client leaves feeling rejuvenated and confident. With a commitment to environmentally friendly practices, Sleek Care combines modern aesthetics with a sustainable ethos, all while providing convenient parking for a seamless experience.
