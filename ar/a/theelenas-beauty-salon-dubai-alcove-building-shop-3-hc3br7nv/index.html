About

At TheElenas beauty salon, clients experience a serene escape where beauty meets relaxation. Specializing in Japanese pedicures and luxurious gelish treatments, the salon offers a range of services designed to pamper and rejuvenate. With a commitment to excellence and a woman-owned ethos, visitors are treated to a personalized experience complemented by convenient amenities like accessible parking and proximity to public transport. Each of our masters is proficient in modern techniques and approaches their work with warmth and care. Welcome to try our services 💚
