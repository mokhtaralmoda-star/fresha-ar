About

Unknotted transforms the ordinary into a sanctuary of relaxation, specializing in a diverse range of massage therapies that cater to unique individual needs. Their offerings include everything from nurturing pre and postnatal massages to deeply restorative Yin Breathwork and guided meditation sessions. With a commitment to using only organic and vegan products, visitors can indulge in self-care while enjoying amenities like refreshing showers and bath towels for a truly revitalizing experience.
