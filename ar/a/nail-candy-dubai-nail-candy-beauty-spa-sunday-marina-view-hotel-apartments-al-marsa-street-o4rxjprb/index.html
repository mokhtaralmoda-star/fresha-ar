About

At Nail Candy, you can relax while we take care of your beauty needs. Enjoy the inviting atmosphere and let us look after you. With many years of experience and our promise of excellent customer service, it’s not just our job—it’s our passion! Our dedicated team of nail technicians is here to welcome and assist you. Located in Dubai Marina, Nail Candy offers free parking next to our salon. We provide a variety of services, including manicures, pedicures, gel and acrylic nail extensions, threading, and massages. To learn more, please visit our 'services' section or give us a call.
