About

In the heart of Muscat, RAZ Salon offers a refined selection of beauty services designed to enhance your natural beauty. Specializing in microblading, combo brows, and ombre powder techniques, their skilled team ensures a flawless finish every time. Indulge in a relaxing manicure and pedicure, or elevate your look with expertly applied eyelash extensions, all tailored to meet your personal style.
