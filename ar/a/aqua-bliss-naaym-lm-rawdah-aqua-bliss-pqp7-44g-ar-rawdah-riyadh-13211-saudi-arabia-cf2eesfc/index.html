About

Aqua Bliss offers a serene escape into the world of luxurious self-care, perfect for those seeking rejuvenation and relaxation. With a menu that includes traditional Moroccan baths, both classic and herbal, along with pampering manicure and pedicure services, the salon embodies the essence of tranquility. Clients can indulge in a royal experience that revitalizes the body and soothes the soul, making every visit a delightful retreat.
