About

Dorh Spa is your ultimate beauty salon! Located in the heart of Riyadh in the vibrant Al Yarmouk district, our premium destination is perfectly situated and easily accessible for everyone. If you are searching for a luxurious women spa near you, look no further. We are a high-end, integrated beauty care spa dedicated to excellence. As the premier nail hair salon near you, we provide comprehensive hair care and stunning nail artistry. Are you looking for a top-tier nail salon? Our team features a highly creative and professional nail stylist ready to transform your look. For those seeking the best manicure places near you, we guarantee a flawless, elegant finish. Beyond aesthetics, our beauty salon offers an oasis of pure relaxation. Indulge in our authentic Moroccan bath to rejuvenate your skin, or unwind with our extensive massage services, including a specialized lymphatic massage tailored specifically for women. Experience true luxury today. Whether you desire a fresh hairstyle or a soothing escape, our beauty salon delivers unmatched perfection. Step into our beauty salon and let our expert team pamper you from head to toe.
