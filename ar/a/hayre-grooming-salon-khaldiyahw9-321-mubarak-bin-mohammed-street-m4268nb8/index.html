About

HAYRE Grooming Salon is a premium men's grooming salon in Al Danah, Abu Dhabi (United Square, near Hamdan Street). We specialise in precision men's haircuts, skin fades and tapers, beard grooming and hot towel shaves — and every haircut includes a wash, hot towel and styling. Beyond the chair, our menu covers men's facials and deep-cleansing skin treatments, anti-hair-fall and scalp detox rituals, keratin and protein smoothing, professional hair colour for men, and kids' haircuts. Expect a sleek, spotless space, skilled barbers, easy parking and doors open daily until 11pm. Walk in or book online in seconds — men's grooming in Abu Dhabi, done properly.
