About

At KOK Studio, grooming is elevated to an art form, where skilled barbers provide tailored haircuts and blow dry services that cater exclusively to men. The studio prides itself on being environmentally friendly while ensuring a kid-friendly atmosphere, making it a perfect spot for fathers with young ones. With convenient amenities like parking and proximity to public transport, this is the go-to destination for a polished look in Ismailia.
