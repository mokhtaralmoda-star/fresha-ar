نبذة

With 23 years of experience, Laure’s cutting style is a fusion of barbering and hairdressing techniques, with a strong focus on detail and suitability to the individual. Her approach considers face and head shape, growth patterns and areas of recession to create tailored haircuts that are easy to style and work for the person wearing them. She has particular experience in restyles and receding hairlines, where thoughtful cutting can make all the difference. Known for honest critiques, authentic approach and eye for aesthetics, Laure offers straightforward advice with the aim of helping clients make informed choices about their appearance.
