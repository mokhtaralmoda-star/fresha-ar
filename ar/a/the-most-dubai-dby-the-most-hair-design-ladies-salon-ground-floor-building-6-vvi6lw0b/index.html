About

At The Most Dubai, clients are treated to a sanctuary of relaxation and beauty, where a range of nail services awaits. From classic and Gelish manicures to rejuvenating pedicures and the unique ABC Momento Treatment, every detail is designed to pamper and delight. This woman-owned salon is not only wheelchair accessible but also conveniently located near public transport, ensuring an enjoyable experience for all.
