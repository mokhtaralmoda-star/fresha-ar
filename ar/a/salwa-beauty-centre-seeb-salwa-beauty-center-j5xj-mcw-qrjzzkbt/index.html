About

Al Faye Complex, located in Seeb, offers a luxurious beauty experience at Salwa Beauty Center. Owned by Salwa Al Ghafri, the center specializes in high-end hair, makeup, and body treatments, ensuring a refined and professional atmosphere. Services range from wedding hairstyles to personalized makeup applications, all delivered with attention to detail. With a team of skilled experts and flexible hours, it’s an ideal destination for clients seeking quality care in a relaxing environment.
