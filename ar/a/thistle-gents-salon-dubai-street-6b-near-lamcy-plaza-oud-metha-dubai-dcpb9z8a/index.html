About

Thistle Gents Salon transforms grooming into an art form, where expert barbers create tailored hairstyles that reflect each client's individuality. With a focus on classic and fade haircuts, and indulgent services like hot towel head shaves and rejuvenating hair treatments, clients enjoy an exceptional grooming experience. Stepping into this salon is not just about getting a haircut; it’s about embracing a moment of self-care and relaxation.
