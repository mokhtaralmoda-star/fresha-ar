About

Born in Brussels, Sammy Green is a unique **clean beauty shop & ladies salon** where nature meets science. We carefully select exceptional professional products — from natural and organic formulas to the best of clean beauty innovation — with one priority: beautiful results while caring for your hair, skin and well-being. Discover expert **hair color, highlights, hair treatments and styling**, alongside a complete range of **beauty services**, including manicures, pedicures, waxing, facials, brows and lashes. 📍 Find us on the **Ground Floor of Dubai Hills Mall, next to Centrepoint**. Walk through our beauty shop and discover our **Hidden Gem salon tucked away at the back**. ✨ Come for the beauty. Stay for the experience.
