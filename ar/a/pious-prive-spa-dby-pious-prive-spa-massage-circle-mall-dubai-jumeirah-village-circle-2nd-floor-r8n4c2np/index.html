نبذة

Pious Privé it’s much cooler than your average mall wellness center. It’s actually famous for its "Multi-Universe" concept. Instead of basic white rooms, they have six specific immersive environments: Jungle Room: You feel like you’re in a rainforest. Galaxy Room: Deep darkness and twinkling stars (perfect for a head massage). Ocean Room: Water caustic lighting Cave Room: Grounding, dim amber light, and total silence. Bamboo Room: Minimalist Zen vibes with wood tones. Forest Room: Nature inspired immersive space to bring tranquility and visual textures of a forest indoors. ATTN: Please note that our spa facility does not have an on-site restroom. We kindly recommend using the facilities prior to your arrival located in the mall to ensure your complete comfort during your treatment. Also note that you are reserving a specific appointment slot with an assigned masseuse at Pious Privé, so if you need to cancel your reservation, please do so as early in advance as possible!
