About

Our professional female therapists bring a complete spa experience directly to your home, hotel or private residence, allowing you to relax in total comfort and privacy. Every treatment is thoughtfully tailored to your body, preferred pressure and personal wellness goals. Baliè specialises in lymphatic drainage, body sculpting and slimming treatments designed to help reduce water retention, bloating and swelling while supporting circulation and leaving the body feeling lighter and refreshed. Our complete massage collection also includes: • Deep Tissue Massage • Traditional Balinese Massage • Relaxation Massage • Traditional Thai Massage • Hot Stone Full-Body Massage • Head Massage • Foot and Leg Reflexology • Wood Therapy • Body Reset Treatments • Four-Hands Massage • Couples Massage Whether you are looking to relieve muscle tension, recover after travelling or exercise, reduce puffiness and bloating, improve relaxation or simply enjoy a moment of luxury, Baliè brings a beautifully personalised wellness experience to your door. Luxury home service, delivered with care.
