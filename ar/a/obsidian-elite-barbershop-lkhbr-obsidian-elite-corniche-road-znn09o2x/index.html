About

At Obsidian Elite Barbershop, skilled barbers elevate grooming to an art form, offering an array of services from expertly crafted haircuts to precision beard trims. Clients can enjoy a rejuvenating HydraFacial or indulge in a full wax, ensuring a polished look from head to toe. With a kid-friendly atmosphere and convenient parking, this barbershop is the perfect destination for individuals and families seeking top-tier grooming experiences.
