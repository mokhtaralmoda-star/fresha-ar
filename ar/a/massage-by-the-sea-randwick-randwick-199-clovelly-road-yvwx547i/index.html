Renowned for quality, Massage by the Sea has been a sanctuary for relaxation and healing since 1998. The main clinic in Randwick specialises in qualified remedial massage and offers a diverse array of services, including relaxation, pregnancy and sports massage as well as hot stone therapy, facials and rejuvenating infrared sauna sessions, all delivered in a welcoming environment. With convenient online booking, 7-day week reception, health fund receipts and HICAPS facilities, this woman-owned establishment prioritises the well-being of every client, creating a tranquil retreat amid the hustle and bustle.
