About

Founded by Brent Pankhurst, a visionary barber with a passion for grooming and an unwavering dedication to craftsmanship, Pankhurst London is an award-winning barbershop established in London and now also located at Fairmont the Palm. Our team consists of the finest UK barbers, each with over 15 years of experience in Dubai. Inspired by London’s rich grooming heritage and the iconic style of Savile Row, Pankhurst London sets a new standard in prestigious grooming. Our exclusive Bespoke Chair, made by Bentley for Pankhurst London, adds unparalleled comfort and luxury to your experience.
