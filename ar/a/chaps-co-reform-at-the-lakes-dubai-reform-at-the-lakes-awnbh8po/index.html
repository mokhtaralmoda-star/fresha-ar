About

Chaps and Co - Reform at the Lakes offers more than just a haircut; it's a haven for gentlemen seeking the traditional barbershop experience. Settle into a vintage-style chair, savor an ice-cold drink, and engage in lively conversation while receiving a meticulous haircut and a classic straight shave. It's a place where men can truly be themselves, indulging in timeless grooming rituals amidst a welcoming atmosphere.
