About

At Elie Farah Salon, clients receive exceptional hair and beauty services in a chic, boutique setting. With a focus on personalized care, Elie and Sevy Farah utilize their expertise to create stunning looks, from elegant upstyles for special occasions to vibrant color treatments. The salon is also kid-friendly and offers convenient parking, ensuring a delightful experience for the whole family.
