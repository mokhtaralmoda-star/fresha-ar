About

The Good Man is a barbershop built on the simple idea that some things are better left uncomplicated. We've created a space that brings back the straightforward charm of a traditional barbershop. No gimmicks, no noise, just honest, skilled grooming. This welcoming space offers a full range of services, including back and chest shaves and waxing in a private room. At The Good Man, we believe in the value of small details, good conversation (if that's your thing), and doing our work well. It's a place where you're always welcome, and where you leave looking sharp and feeling like yourself.
