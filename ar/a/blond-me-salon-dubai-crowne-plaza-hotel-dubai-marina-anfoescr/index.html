About

Blond Me Ladies Salon is a brand-new beauty destination located at Marina Promenade, right underneath the Crowne Plaza Hotel. Our talented team of skilled experts is dedicated to providing the highest level of pampering and personalized service. We offer a wide range of hair, nails, and beauty services, including haircuts, coloring, manicures, pedicures, facials, and more. Experience a relaxing atmosphere where your beauty needs are our top priority. We can't wait to welcome you!
