نبذة

Where luxury, expertise, and personal care come together. With 18+ years of professional experience, I specialize in premium blondes, balayage, lived-in colour, precision haircuts, advanced colour corrections, and healthy hair transformations. Every appointment begins with a personalized consultation because beautiful hair starts with the right diagnosis, not just the right formula. I work with professional products including Wella Professionals, K18, Olaplex, Redken, and Moroccanoil to achieve beautiful results while protecting the integrity of your hair. Whether you visit me in the salon or book my Mobile Hair Stylist service, you’ll receive the same professional experience, attention to detail, and personalized care. My goal is simple: to help you leave feeling more confident than when you arrived. ✨ Online booking available ✨ Deposit required to secure your appointment
