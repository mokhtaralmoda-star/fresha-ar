نبذة

Blossom & Berry Beauty Salon is a premium hair and beauty salon in Business Bay, Dubai, specialising in advanced hair restoration, Japanese hair treatments, Tokio Inkarami and luxury Head Spa rituals. Discover professional solutions for dry, damaged, frizzy and chemically treated hair, including intensive hair repair, deep conditioning and personalised scalp care. Our signature Tokio Inkarami treatments combine innovative Japanese haircare technology with carefully selected professional formulas to improve hair softness, strength, shine and manageability. Enjoy relaxing Japanese Head Spa experiences, scalp detox treatments, expert haircuts, hair colouring, balayage, blonding and professional styling. Our salon also offers manicures, pedicures, facials and selected beauty treatments. Located in Sobha Ivory 1, Business Bay, Blossom & Berry offers a calm, private, hijab-friendly environment, personalised consultations and convenient free parking. Experience thoughtful beauty care, professional expertise and a little everyday luxury.
