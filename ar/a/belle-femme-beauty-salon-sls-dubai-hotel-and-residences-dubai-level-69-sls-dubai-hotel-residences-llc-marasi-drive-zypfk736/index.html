About

Belle Femme Hair and Nail Lounge is the first in the Middle East to implement "Salon Emotion" by L'Oréal, an innovative concept designed to transform visits to the hairdressers. Located in SLS Dubai Hotel & Residences, Belle Femme Hair and Nail Lounge is where clients can relax and unwind in an attentive environment and have an enjoyable experience that has an emotional impact. From service to design, Salon Emotion focuses on a more 'emotional' approach–appealing to the senses and the needs of the client from when she first enters the salon to the moment she leaves, looking forward to return.
