Brown Sugar Rozelle offers a more personal take on beauty appointments, with brow work and waxing that clients describe as thorough and gentle. The brow artists shape and thread eyebrows, while the team also provides brow lamination, Brazilian waxing and full-body spray tans; first-time tanning clients say the process is explained before, during and after the appointment.
