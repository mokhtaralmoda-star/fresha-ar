About

HISMA Spa offers a luxurious retreat with a traditional Saudi interior inspired by the famous Hisma (حسمى) Mountains in Tabuk. The spa provides a range of services including massages, nail care, and basic hair treatments. With its serene ambiance and skilled therapists, HISMA Spa delivers a unique and relaxing experience that honors Saudi heritage while catering to modern wellness needs.
