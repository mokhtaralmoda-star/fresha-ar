About

At Kay Beauty Atelier, clients are enveloped in a serene atmosphere dedicated to enhancing beauty and self-care. This women-only salon specializes in a variety of services, from vibrant highlights to other luxury hair services, nails, waxing, threading, massages, facials, brows and lashes, ensuring a tailored experience that caters to individual preferences. With a focus on quality and attention to detail, each appointment promises a rejuvenating escape, leaving guests feeling confident and revitalized. We deliver the quality and the well known brands (K18, Kerastase, Schwarzkopf, Essie, Nano and The Gel Factory) on our clients with our highly trained staff. We create what you imagine!
