About

A luxurious sanctuary that caters to all your beauty needs in a truly relaxing environment, Amaya Salon and Spa can transform and rejuvenate you from head to toe. Amaya Salon and Spa put you first, delivering trendsetting style, creativity, and tailor-made services. Our professional team of artists and experts is always on hand to offer consultation, make suitable recommendations and of course turn your beauty vision into a glamourous reality. We offer top quality services such as Haircuts, colouring, highlights, lowlights, straightening, extensions, and keratin treatment.
