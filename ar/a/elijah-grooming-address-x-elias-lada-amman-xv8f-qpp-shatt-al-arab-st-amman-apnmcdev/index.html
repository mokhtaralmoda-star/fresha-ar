About

ELIJAH Grooming Address is a premium men’s grooming salon and barbershop in Amman, Jordan, founded by Elias Lada. Specializing in precision haircuts, expert barbering, beard grooming, shaving, skincare, and personalized service, ELIJAH combines exceptional craftsmanship and attention to detail with a refined, contemporary atmosphere. Located in Um Uthaina, Amman, ELIJAH is a destination for men seeking professional barbering and an elevated grooming experience in Jordan. More than a destination—it’s your grooming address.
