نبذة

A private, one-on-one studio specialising in Brazilian + Body Waxing, where your appointment is never rushed and every detail is thoughtfully considered ✨ With 15+ years of industry experience and a genuine passion for waxing, I’ve created a space where expertise, comfort and consistency come together. I’ve built a loyal, repeat clientele who trust my precision, professionalism and judgement-free approach. From the moment you arrive, you’ll have my undivided attention, with time to understand your needs and what works best for you. Your comfort, modesty and privacy are always prioritised, with every treatment performed with care and precision. Brazilian waxing is performed using premium hard wax and a considered technique to deliver a consistently smooth result while caring for your skin. From the products used to the way each wax is performed, every detail has a purpose. Alongside waxing, I offer natural-looking brows and professional spray tanning, tailored to complement your features. For the woman who values consistency, attention to detail and having someone who knows her wax, remembers the little things and delivers the result she came for every time. You’ve found your girl💖
