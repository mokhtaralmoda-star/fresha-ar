نبذة

Founded by Christian Alexander, a Paris-trained hairstylist and makeup artist with nearly 30 years of editorial, touring and celebrity work, The Atelier recreates the private, one-on-one experience he spent his career delivering in hotel suites. Together with his partner, Nurse Simon Reader-Alexander, he created a hair and aesthetics studio in a former furniture workshop in Fortitude Valley's historic Bell Bros. Building. We don't believe in surface-level beauty. Healthy hair and skin reflect what's happening within, and that's where our work begins: hair, skin and wellbeing as one conversation, in a space designed entirely around you.
