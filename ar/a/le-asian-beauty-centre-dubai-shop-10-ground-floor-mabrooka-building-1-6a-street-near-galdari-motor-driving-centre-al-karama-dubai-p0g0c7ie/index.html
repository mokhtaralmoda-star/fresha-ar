About

At Le Asian Beauty Centre, luxurious hair transformations await, tailored exclusively for women. With a commitment to environmentally friendly practices, the salon offers an array of services, including Hair Henna, Protein Treatments, and vibrant Highlights. Conveniently located with available parking and close to public transport, clients can indulge in a rejuvenating experience while enhancing their natural beauty.
