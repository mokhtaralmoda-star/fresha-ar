نبذة

BodySpace Recovery Studio – your private wellness, recovery and body transformation destination in Jandakot. We believe recovery is for everybody. Whether you're managing pain, recovering from injury or surgery, training for your next event, supporting your lymphatic health, or simply taking time for yourself, we're here to help you feel your best. Our experienced team offers Remedial, Deep Tissue, Relaxation and Pregnancy Massage, Manual Lymphatic Drainage, Body Sculpting including Fat Cavitation, RF Skin Tightening and Vacuum Therapy, Wellness & Recovery treatments including Body Roll, Infrared Wellness Pod and Compression Boots, plus Reiki, Chakra Balance and Lymphatic Sculpting Facial Massage. Every treatment takes place in a private, calming space and is tailored to your individual goals. From reducing stress and easing muscle tension to improving circulation, supporting recovery and enhancing confidence, we're passionate about helping you move, feel and live better. BodySpace Recovery Studio isn't just a massage clinic—it's a complete wellness destination where recovery, relaxation and results come together.
