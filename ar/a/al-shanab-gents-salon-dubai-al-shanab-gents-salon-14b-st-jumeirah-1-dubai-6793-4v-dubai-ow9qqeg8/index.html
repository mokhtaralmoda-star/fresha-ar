About

Located beside Seddiqi mosque behind Mercato mall, Al Shanab Gents Salon is the latest craze among the men of Dubai. It is the perfect blend of a classic old-school Emarati barbershop in a modern setting. We offer professional services from traditional blade shaves to taper fade haircuts, book your appointment today to experience some of Dubai’s finest barbers.
