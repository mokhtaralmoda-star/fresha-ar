About

The Galleria Hair Beauty Lounge is a sanctuary of elegance and sophistication, redefining beauty in the heart of Dubai. Located at the Avani+ Palm View Hotel & Suites, we are dedicated to delivering a world-class experience that blends artistry, innovation, and unparalleled care. At The Galleria, we celebrate individuality and timeless beauty. Our mission is to empower every guest to feel confident, radiant, and truly unique. Open to all, we provide a space where luxury meets self-expression, creating moments of indulgence and transformation.
