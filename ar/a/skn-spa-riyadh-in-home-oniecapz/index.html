About

SKN is your sanctuary for beauty and calm, offering luxury in-home services in Riyadh. Discover thoughtfully curated treatments, from meticulous nail care and beautifully crafted lashes to restorative massages and traditional hammam rituals. Attentive care and an eye for detail bring a moment of calm to the comfort of your home, allowing you to slow down and feel renewed. Your comfort is at the heart of every experience.
