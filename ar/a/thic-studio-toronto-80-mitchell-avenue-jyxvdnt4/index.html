نبذة

Founded by celebrated hairstylist Morgan Tully, THIC Studio has become one of Toronto’s premier destinations for exceptional hair and a luxury salon experience. Home to a talented team of highly skilled stylists and colourists, THIC Studio brings together individual expertise, creativity, and a shared passion for beautiful, healthy hair. From precision cutting and effortless, lived-in colour to transformative blonding, our team is dedicated to creating looks that feel modern, elevated, and uniquely you. With a reputation for exceptional service, ongoing education, and attention to detail, THIC Studio offers a welcoming, collaborative environment where every guest feels taken care of. Set within a thoughtfully designed, light-filled space, THIC Studio is more than a salon. It’s a destination for great hair, genuine connection, and an experience worth returning for.
