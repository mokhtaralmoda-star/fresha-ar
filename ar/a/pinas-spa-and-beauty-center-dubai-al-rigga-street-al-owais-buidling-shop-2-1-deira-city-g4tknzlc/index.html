About

At Pinas Spa and Beauty Center, guests can immerse themselves in a serene oasis designed for relaxation and rejuvenation. Specializing in a variety of services from indulgent pedicure packages to specialized hand and foot treatments, the spa is committed to using organic and vegan products, ensuring an environmentally friendly experience. With a focus on inclusivity and comfort, this woman-owned and Asian-owned haven welcomes everyone, providing wheelchair accessibility and a kid-friendly environment.
