نبذة

Vigorous Therapeutic Massage brings authentic Thai massage to Melbourne CBD at 391 Bourke Street. Established in 2005, our experienced therapists specialise in deep tissue massage, traditional Thai massage, foot massage & reflexology, relaxation massage, and area-specific treatments for neck, shoulders and back. We customise every session to suit your body's needs — from gentle relaxation to firm, therapeutic deep tissue work. Our clean, modern studio offers a calm escape from the bustle of the CBD, with same-day bookings and walk-ins always welcome.
