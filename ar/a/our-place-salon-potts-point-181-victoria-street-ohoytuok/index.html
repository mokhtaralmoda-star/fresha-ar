نبذة

On a historical, tree-lined, bustling street in Potts Point is Our Place Salon, which has become the hairdressing salon to suit the lifestyle of the busy executive through to the fashion savvy local. Opened in 2010 by celebrated hairstylists Kelly Grant and Clive Allwright. The salon houses an endearing and homely contemporary space, as Grant and Allwright wanted to move away from the bigger and corporate style of salon. The salons tag line says it all. “The place for exceptional hairdressing” Our Place Salon offers highly elevated craft in a laid-back professional environment.
