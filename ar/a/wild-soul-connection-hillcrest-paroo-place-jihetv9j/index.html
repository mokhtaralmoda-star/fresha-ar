نبذة

Wild Soul Connection is a space created for movement, relaxation, self-care and connection. From personalised massage and beauty treatments to personal training, sound bowl sessions and wellness rituals, each experience is designed to help you feel more relaxed, supported and connected to yourself. Laurinda also brings Wild Soul Connection beyond the treatment room through corporate massage and workplace wellness, helping business bring a little relaxation and self care into the workday. Whether you are looking to ease tired muscles, build strength and fitness, enjoy some quiet time for yourself or create wellness experiences for your team, you will receive a warm personal approach and genuine care. Move. Breath. Connect
