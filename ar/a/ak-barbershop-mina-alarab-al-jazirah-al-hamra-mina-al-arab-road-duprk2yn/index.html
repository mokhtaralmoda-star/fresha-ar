About

AK Barbershop offers a tailored gentlemen's grooming experience in a traditional-style barbershop, providing an opportunity for men to relax while receiving a clean haircut and a straight shave. Situated in the heart of the Mina Alarab waterfront community in Ras al Khaima, our services are meticulously tailored to meet your needs. Our comprehensive services include hair and beard cuts, trims, color treatments, pedicures, and manicures, with additional options such as facials and grooming enhancements.
