نبذة

​From M Tokyo is a nail salon that breaks away from the norm found in Toronto. What makes us different from the rest is the way we present the whole nail experience. The necessity of delivering top quality service, comfort, and privacy are fundamentals of the status quo found in Japan, where this type of Nail fashion originates. One can get 'Japanese style' nails in Toronto, or one can get true authenticity at From M Tokyo. ​We are owned and operated by skilled and experienced technicians from Japan. We use top grade materials and the latest trends that the Japanese nail industry has to offer.
