About

CAREVO Home Beauty and Wellness offers a serene escape where clients can indulge in a variety of rejuvenating treatments. Specializing in classic and deep tissue massages, as well as unique services like warts removal and eyebrow tinting, the team prioritizes organic products for a holistic experience. With an adults-only ambiance and convenient access to public transport, CAREVO is dedicated to enhancing well-being in a tranquil setting.
