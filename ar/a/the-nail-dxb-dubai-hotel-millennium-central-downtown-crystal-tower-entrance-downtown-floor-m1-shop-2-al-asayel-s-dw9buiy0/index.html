نبذة

The Nail DXB specializes in Russian techniques for Nails • Lashes • Brows, nestled in the heart of Business Bay & Downtown Dubai. An intimate space designed for calm, comfort, and quiet luxury, where every detail is considered. Our Russian-certified masters specialise in precision techniques that enhance natural beauty, while upholding the highest standards of hygiene, nail health, and skin care. From signature Russian manicures, BIAB, gel extensions and nail art, to Japanese strengthening therapies, anti-ageing rituals, and Moroccan Hammam hand & foot treatments.
