About

Lclassicobarbers is one of the best professional East African barbers in Oman, known for its affordable prices and expertise. Specializing in fade cuts, dreadlocks, and a variety of styles for both youth and elders, it is located in the heart of Seeb Alkhoud 7. The barbershop is a go-to spot for anyone looking for quality service and a skilled touch, ensuring every customer leaves satisfied. Whether you’re after a fresh fade or looking to try something new, Lclassicobarbers is dedicated to delivering the best barbering experience.
