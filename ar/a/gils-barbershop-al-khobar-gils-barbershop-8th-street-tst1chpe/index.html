نبذة

At Gil’s, we honor the timeless craft of classic barbering — refined by Master Barber Gilbert’s 20+ years of experience in Bahrain’s most prestigious hotels. While trends come and go, we focus on precision cuts, beard trims, and styling that stand the test of time. Inspired by European charm, our barbershop blends red brick walls, rich green tones, and Italian chairs with cinematic portraits of Hollywood icons. More than grooming, Gil’s offers an experience of craftsmanship, comfort, and character — where every detail reflects style and story.
