About

The Hound isn't just a place to get a haircut or a shave. It's the hub of community – where people get together to connect, exchange ideas, just hang and feel like themselves. We created the Hound to do just that, provide high quality experiences and product to make you feel like the best of you The Hound is a neighbourhood barbershop. Always has been, always will be. As we evolve and grow our community, we continue to take a block-by-block approach to everything we do. Our neighbourhood roots inform and inspire our spaces, products and practices. Our community sets us apart.
