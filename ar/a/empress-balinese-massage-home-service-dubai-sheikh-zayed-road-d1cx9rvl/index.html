About

Luxury massage at home in Dubai, and the city's go-to specialists for prenatal and postnatal massage. Empress Balinese Massage brings the full spa set-up to your door: massage table, fresh linens, oils and calming music, arranged for comfortable side-lying massage during pregnancy. Our therapists bring an average of 15 years 5-star hotel experience to every session. Prenatal massage from 20 weeks eases back and hip pain, sciatica, swollen ankles and tired legs. Postnatal recovery massage helps new mums release the strain of feeding and carrying, with baby close by at home. We use pregnancy-safe oils only. We also offer Balinese, deep tissue, hot stone, lymphatic drainage, sports and couples massage. No travel, no traffic. Afterwards you stay in your own calm space instead of losing that post-massage zen on the way home. Rated 5.0 from over 1,500 reviews. Open daily 10am–11pm across Dubai. Perfect as a babymoon treat or a gift for a new mum, pregnant mother or a gift for your wife.
