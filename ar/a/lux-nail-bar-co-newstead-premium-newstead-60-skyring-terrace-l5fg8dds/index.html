نبذة

Premium Lux Nail Bar & Co in Newstead is a modern, high-end nail salon dedicated to delivering luxury beauty experiences in a relaxing and stylish setting. We specialise in BIAB, acrylic extensions, custom nail art, luxury manicures and spa pedicures, using high-quality products to ensure long-lasting results. Our experienced technicians focus on precision, hygiene and personalised service, making every visit comfortable and indulgent. Located in the heart of Newstead, we are committed to helping you look and feel your absolute best.
