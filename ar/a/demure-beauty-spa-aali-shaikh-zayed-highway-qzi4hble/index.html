نبذة

Demure Salon & Spa is a refined beauty destination where elegance meets intention. Created for women who value calm, quality, and subtle luxury, Demure offers a serene space designed for comfort and care. Our services include professional hair styling and treatments, manicure and pedicure, authentic Moroccan bath rituals, and relaxing massage therapies — all delivered with precision, warmth, and attention to detail. At Demure, beauty is never rushed. Every visit is a moment to pause, unwind, and leave feeling confident, refreshed, and beautifully renewed.
