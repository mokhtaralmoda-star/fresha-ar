About

VLT Barber House brings precision and care to every appointment, from sharp skin and taper fades to neatly shaped, colour-refreshed beards. The menu also includes haircuts for children under 12, alongside express and deep-cleansing facials for a fresh, well-groomed finish. Whether you’re after a quick tidy-up or a more considered refresh, the team is ready to help.
