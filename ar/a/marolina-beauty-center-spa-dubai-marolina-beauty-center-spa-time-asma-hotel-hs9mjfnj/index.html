About

“Do it, with love” has been the principle of Marolina beauty center and spa since its inception in 2021. Founded by Adel, the salon aims to bring a sense of inclusion, confidence, and positivity to each client that walks through its doors. Marolina Beauty Center and spa is home to over 15 stylists that hold these ideals close to heart, naturally creating an uplifting environment both internally and in the local beauty industry. With services for men and women Beauty center & spa provides genuine care for each person that sits in the chair while enhancing their natural beauty, inside and out.
