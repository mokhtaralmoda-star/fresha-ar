About

The Groom Room Barber Shop is an upscale, bespoke gentleman's barber shop that offers today's progressive gentlemen, a haven where he can sit back, relax, and experience the "world Class" art of grooming and services in a very relaxed atmosphere, which is masculine and therapeutic.
