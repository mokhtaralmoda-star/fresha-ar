About

Discover luxury at Elata Beauty in Sunset Mall. Immerse yourself in a haven of indulgence with a variety of top-notch beauty services. Our Hair Station features L’Oréal and Kérastase products, while the Facial Room showcases Guinot expertise. The Make-Up Studio exclusively uses M.A.C Cosmetics, where our talented artists create magic. For multitasking mavens, our Portable Nail Station is a mani/pedi paradise. Experience smooth perfection in the Waxing Room with Depileve. Trust our friendly team of professional hairdressers and therapists for a pampering session filled with quality and care.
