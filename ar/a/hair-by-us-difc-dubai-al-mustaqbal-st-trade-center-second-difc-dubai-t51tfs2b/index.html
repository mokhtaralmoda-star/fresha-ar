About

Hair By Us - DIFC stands out in Dubai's vibrant salon scene with its commitment to artistry and precision in hair care. Offering a range of services from elegant Ice Gel Polish to soft gel extensions and French applications, this salon is dedicated to helping clients achieve their desired looks effortlessly. Whether opting for BIAB or stylish fake nails, clients can expect a professional and welcoming atmosphere for an elevated beauty experience.
