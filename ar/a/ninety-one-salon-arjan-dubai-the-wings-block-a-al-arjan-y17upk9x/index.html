نبذة

NINETY ONE is a vibrant and friendly hair, nails and beauty salon in the heart of Arjan, Dubai. Home to a talented team of British, Irish and international hair professionals, specialising in expert hair colouring, blondes, balayage, precision cutting, hair extensions, we also have excellent team of nail technicians and beauty treatments. We pride ourselves on exceptional results, a welcoming atmosphere and a personal touch. Expect a lively salon with plenty of personality, friendly faces, great conversations and a real home away from home feeling. Beautiful hair, great energy and a salon you'll love coming back to!
