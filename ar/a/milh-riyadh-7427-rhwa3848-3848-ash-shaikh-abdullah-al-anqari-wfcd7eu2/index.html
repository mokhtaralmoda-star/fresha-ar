About

Milh is a beauty and wellness space in Riyadh where you can do your nails, lashes, massage, Japanese head spa, and Gua Sha facials all in one place. We focus on clean, high-quality techniques including Japanese manicure and eco gel options. During your appointment, you can relax with guided meditation or enjoy a sound bath through headsets while getting your nails or lashes done — turning your visit into a calming, feel-good experience, not just a service. The space itself is bold, colorful, and maximalist — Milh is anti-beige, designed to feel expressive, alive, and anything but ordinary.
