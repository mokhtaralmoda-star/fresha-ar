About

Unique Station Beauty Salon is a beautiful, girly and picture-perfect beauty destination where beauty, glamour, pampering and fun come together! 🎀✨ Our salon is designed with a feminine, luxurious and Instagram-worthy aesthetic, making it the perfect place for beauty treatments, girls’ days, celebrations and unforgettable memories. We specialise in kids pamper parties, princess parties and girls’ pamper packages, creating magical experiences for birthdays, special occasions and celebrations. Our little guests can enjoy a fun and exciting pamper experience with their friends while feeling like a princess for the day. 👑🎀 Our beauty services include quality gel nails, acrylic nails, manicures, facials, lashes and relaxing massages, with pamper packages available for children, teens and adults. Whether you want glamorous nails, beautiful lashes, a relaxing facial or a full pamper day, we have something to make you feel fabulous.
