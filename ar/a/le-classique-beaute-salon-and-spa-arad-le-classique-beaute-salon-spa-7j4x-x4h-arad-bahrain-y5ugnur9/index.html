About

Le Classique Beauty Salon & Spa is where beauty, elegance, and relaxation come together. We offer a carefully curated range of beauty and wellness services, including expert hair styling and colouring, nourishing hair treatments, nail care, facials, massage, and traditional Moroccan baths. With a focus on personalised care, attention to detail, and a welcoming atmosphere, we strive to make every visit a truly special experience, leaving every client feeling confident, refreshed, and beautiful.
