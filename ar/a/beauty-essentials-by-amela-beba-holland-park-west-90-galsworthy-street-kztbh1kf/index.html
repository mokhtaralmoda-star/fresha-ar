For 11 years, Beauty Essentials by Amela (BEBA) has been a trusted beauty salon located in Holland Park West, Brisbane. We are passionate about providing personalised beauty, skin and body treatments in a welcoming and professional environment. Our services include customised facials, microneedling, IPL skin rejuvenation, pigmentation and vascular treatments, radio frequency, LED therapy, lash extensions, lash lifts, brow styling, waxing, spray tans, body sculpting and our specialised TESLA Chair & Former for pelvic floor strengthening and body toning. At BEBA, we combine quality treatments, advanced technology and personalised care to help you look and feel your best.
