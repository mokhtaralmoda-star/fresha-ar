نبذة

If you suffer from respiratory or skin conditions, Salt Therapy provides an all-natural, drug-free & non-invasive solution that has been proven for centuries to cleanse, clear mucus, kill bacteria & viruses, & reduce inflammation throughout the entire respiratory system. When salt particles are inhaled the mucus starts to break down making it much easier to remove through coughing. This will improve your immune system & also provide a general sense of well being. Salt therapy is also proven beneficial in the treatment of a number of skin conditions including eczema, psoriasis & dermatitis.
