About

Salon de Riverine is a luxury ladies salon and wellness destination in Dubai that combines high-quality services with a commitment to premium care and client satisfaction. Salon de Riverine was created to redefine the beauty experience by showing that elegance, wellness, and advanced treatments can coexist seamlessly. Rooted in a philosophy that true beauty comes from confidence and care, the salon proudly uses international, premium products and cutting-edge techniques. With a skilled team of hairdressers, nail artists, lash specialists, and skincare experts, Salon de Riverine delivers exceptional services while providing a serene and luxurious environment. Salon de Riverine more than a ladies salon, a place to feel radiant and confident.
