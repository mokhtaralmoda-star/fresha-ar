نبذة

LIMBR is a boutique wellness centre in Seef, Bahrain, bringing movement, recovery and self-care together in one welcoming and peaceful space. Our services include personalised assisted stretching, massage, facials and face sculpting, alongside movement classes, personal training, infrared sauna, cold plunge and flotation. Whether you’re easing everyday tension, building a movement routine or simply taking time to unwind, our team helps you find the right combination for your needs. With individual treatments and thoughtfully paired experiences, LIMBR makes it easier to move better, feel better and make time for yourself.
