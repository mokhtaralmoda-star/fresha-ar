About

Soul Shine Ladies Salon is a sanctuary for women seeking a range of hair and body treatments in a serene environment. With services including full body waxing, underarm and leg treatments, clients can expect meticulous care tailored to their needs. Conveniently located near public transport with parking available, this salon ensures a welcoming atmosphere that caters to women exclusively, making it a perfect choice for a rejuvenating experience.
