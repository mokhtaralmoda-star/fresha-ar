About

At Crowns and Petals Beauty Salon, we offer quiet luxury for the woman who already feels beautiful – we just make your glow last longer. Experience authentic Japanese head-spa rituals, Elemis Pro-Collagen facials, and our signature Crowns and Petals Style colour by our “colour scientist.” A serene escape with free parking and kid-friendly pampering, designed for women who value calm, quality, and care in every detail.
