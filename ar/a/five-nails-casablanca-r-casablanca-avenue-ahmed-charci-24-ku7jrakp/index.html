About

Five Nails Casablanca ® Next-generation nail studio in Morocco. A 100% nail-focused studio, open to all genders, specialized in Gel-X (Aprés Nails) — our signature — as well as BIAB (The GelBottle), semi-permanent, pedicure, and signature nail art. We work exclusively with Aprés Nails and only with TPO & HEMA-free products, offering 600+ colors and finishes for full customization. Clean finishes, long-lasting results, and controlled timing. Nails that express who you are.
