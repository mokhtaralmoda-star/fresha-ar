نبذة

Blade Beauty Boutique is a modern beauty collective in collaboration with Auréa Haus, bringing together passionate specialists under one roof at the corner of Queen & Roncesvalles. From advanced laser and skin treatments to cosmetic injectables, spray tanning, scalp micropigmentation, nails, and beauty services, every treatment is performed by a technician who specializes in their craft. We believe expertise matters, and our clients deserve the best. At Blade, you’ll find advanced technology, personalized treatment plans, and a welcoming space where results, education, and confidence come first. Whether you’re visiting us for the first time or have been with us for years, our goal is simple: to help you look and feel your absolute best. Because great results start with the right specialist.
