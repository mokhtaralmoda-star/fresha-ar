نبذة

Moxa Wellness is a calm and modern wellness destination in downtown Toronto, conveniently located near King & Spadina, The Well, Rogers Centre and the Entertainment District. We offer signature massage, foot massage, reflexology, Registered Massage Therapy, acupuncture, manicure, pedicure and waxing services in a comfortable and thoughtfully designed setting. For insurance-eligible massage, select the “Registered Massage Therapy (RMT)” category on Fresha. Appointments with additional RMTs are available through our website at moxatoronto.com. Direct billing is available for eligible insurance plans. Acupuncture and Traditional Chinese Medicine appointments are also available through our website. We are open daily from 9 AM to 9 PM. Same-day appointments and walk-ins are available, subject to availability.
