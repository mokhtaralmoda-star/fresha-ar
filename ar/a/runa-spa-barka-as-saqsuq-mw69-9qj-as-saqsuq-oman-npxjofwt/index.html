About

Runa Spa Barka stands out as a premier hair salon that specializes in a range of threading services and hair loss treatments tailored to enhance your natural beauty. With a focus on meticulous techniques for eyebrows, upper lip, and face threading, every visit promises a rejuvenating experience. Conveniently located with available parking, Runa Spa Barka is dedicated to providing exceptional care in a welcoming atmosphere for all clients.
