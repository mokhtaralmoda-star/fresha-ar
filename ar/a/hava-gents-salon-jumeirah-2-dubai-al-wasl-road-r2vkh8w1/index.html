نبذة

Hava Gents Salon Jumeirah 2 is at Villa 1, Al Wasl Road, Dubai. We offer haircuts, beard grooming, colour and hair care, facials, massage, nail and foot care, Moroccan bath, waxing and IPL hair reduction. Organic and environmentally friendly products are available. Wi-Fi and free customer parking are available, with paid public street parking nearby. Open daily from 9:30 am to midnight.
