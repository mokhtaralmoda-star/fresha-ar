About

At Salonnajla, the focus is on providing luxurious hair and nail services while prioritizing sustainability and the use of organic products. This woman-owned salon in Beirut offers a range of treatments, including exquisite Russian manicures and pedicures, as well as hair extensions removal. With amenities like parking, sauna access, and convenient public transport nearby, clients can indulge in a rejuvenating experience that leaves them feeling pampered and refreshed.
