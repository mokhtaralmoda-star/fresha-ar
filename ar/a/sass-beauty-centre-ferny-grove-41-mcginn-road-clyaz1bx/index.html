نبذة

Sass Beauty Centre (SBC) is founded on the desire to provide exceptional results across both skin and beauty services. SBC is an established beauty centre with a genuine passion for great skin, quality treatments and creating an experience our clients genuinely look forward to. Our commitment to what we do has been recognised within the industry, with SBC proudly named among Queensland’s leading salons at the Australian Beauty Industry Awards over the years. Our philosophy is simple: never stop learning. Our experienced therapists continually develop their knowledge and skills to bring you professional advice, quality treatments and real results. From advanced skin treatments and Dermalogica facials to massage, brows, lashes, waxing and cosmetic tattooing, you'll find all your beauty favourites under one roof. We believe a positive salon starts with a positive team, so we've purposefully created a culture where our therapists feel supported, valued and encouraged to grow. We think our clients can feel that too — in the warm welcome, the laughs, the genuine care and the way our team works together to make every visit one you look forward to.
