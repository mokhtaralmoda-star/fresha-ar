نبذة

Soluxe Skin House is a premier aesthetics sanctuary located at 1587 Sandgate Road, where world-class dermal science meets clinical expertise. Our boutique clinic offers a private, sophisticated environment led by a multidisciplinary team: Jo (Nurse Practitioner Candidate), Adriana (Brazilian-trained Dermatologist & Aesthetic Specialist), and Dr. Albert Ho (Cosmetic Physician). With over 30 years of international experience, we specialise in regenerative aesthetics and clinical dermatology. We provide evidence-based care for wrinkle treatments, facial volume loss, and skin longevity using advanced medical-grade technologies, including Ultraformer III HIFU, Exosome therapy, and Polynucleotides. Beyond aesthetics, we are a trusted provider of comprehensive skin cancer screening and dermoscopy for the North Brisbane community. Our expertise covers the clinical management of acne, rosacea, and pigmentation, alongside therapeutic interventions for TMJ, hyperhidrosis, and women’s skin health. Experience global expertise and doctor-led care in our Nundah sanctuary.
