About

MAYA ladies Beauty Salon at The Myriad Dubai, focused on university students. Your one-stop destination for all of your hair, nail and body treatments. You will be welcomed with a friendly, warm and energetic vibe! Services will include manicure, pedicure, hair styling including Afro hair specialist, waxing, threading, massage and more! Depending on your time and budget, you can select from mini and quick treatments or longer ones, the choice is yours!
