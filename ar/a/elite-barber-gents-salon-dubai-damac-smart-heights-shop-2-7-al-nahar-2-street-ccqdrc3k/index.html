About

Elite Barber, conveniently located in Barsha Heights, has been keeping men handsome since 2021 with bespoke male grooming and haircut services. With a perfectionist mentality, we blend excellent service with the latest style trends, delivered by our experienced and talented staff. We strive to make each experience as unique and authentic as the individuals who fill our shop. We provide a comprehensive menu of services for men, including haircuts, straight razor shaves, and our signature massage shampoo. Every service is complemented by relaxing shoulder massage, ensuring a a great experience.
