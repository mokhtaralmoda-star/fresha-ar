About

BERYOZKA — the first official certified Russian beauty space to introduce LED lash extensions in Morocco. We are not just a beauty salon. We are also an official Russian beauty academy in Morocco, and our founder is an international lash trainer. LED lashes are our true specialty. We work with professional certified LED equipment, and every lash artist receives special training before working with clients. Because LED lashes are not simply about buying a lamp — they require real knowledge, correct technique and professional education. We have high standards for everything we do. Every member of our team goes through preparation and training before starting work at BERYOZKA. Alongside our signature LED lash extensions, we also offer professional Russian manicure and pedicure services. At BERYOZKA, you can expect Russian beauty standards, carefully trained specialists, professional techniques and a friendly atmosphere where you can simply relax and enjoy your appointment. We are proud to have been the first to bring professional LED lash technology to Morocco — and even prouder that today so many women trust BERYOZKA with their beauty. 🤍
