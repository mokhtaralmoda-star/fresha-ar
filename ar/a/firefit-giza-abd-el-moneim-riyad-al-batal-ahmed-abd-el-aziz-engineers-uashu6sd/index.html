About

Firefit stands out as a premier destination for fitness and recovery in Giza, offering tailored nutrition plans and dynamic workout programs designed to meet individual goals. With options like the intensive 3-month i-shape and 1-year HIIT training, clients can achieve transformative results in a supportive environment. The facility boasts essential amenities such as parking, showers, and lockers, ensuring a comfortable and convenient experience for every visitor.
