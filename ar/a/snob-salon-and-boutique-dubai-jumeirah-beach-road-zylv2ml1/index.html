About

At Snob Salon and Boutique, beauty transcends the ordinary with an array of services tailored for women. From rejuvenating facials and soothing sugar body scrubs to expert hair styling and indulgent manicures, each treatment reflects the salon's commitment to excellence. With a woman-owned ethos and a team of skilled professionals, clients can expect a personalized experience that enhances their natural beauty in a serene atmosphere.
