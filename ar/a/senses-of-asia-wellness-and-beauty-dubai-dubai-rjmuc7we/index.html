About

Senses of Asia Wellness & Beauty, your serene escape for beauty, wellness and self-care in Dubai. Our experienced beauty specialists and therapists offer a carefully selected range of treatments, including Russian manicure & pedicure, nail enhancements, professional facials, waxing, brows & lashes, relaxing and therapeutic massages, and Hammam rituals. Designed as a peaceful retreat from the city, our salon combines expert techniques, personalized care and a relaxing atmosphere to help you look and feel your best. Complete your experience with our private shower and sauna facilities, and enjoy a moment dedicated entirely to you. Beauty. Wellness. Relaxation — all in one place.
