About

Antibes Healing & Wellness is a luxury wellness destination inspired by the elegance of the French Riviera. We offer therapeutic and recovery-focused treatments, including sports massage, lymphatic drainage, hot stone massage, hijama (Islamic cupping), acupuncture, and other wellbeing services. Our calm, restorative space combines expert care, personalized treatments, and a refined atmosphere to support relaxation, recovery, and overall wellbeing for both men and women.
