Thank you so much for your kind words — I’m really glad you enjoyed the experience and loved the design! 🌿 Just a little note: henna actually takes up to 48 hours to reach its full depth of color after removal! If you need to shower or wash, applying a thin layer of oil or Vaseline over the design beforehand will help protect it. All of this is also outlined on the aftercare card included at the end of your session — give it a read and hopefully your design will darken beautifully! 🤎 Hope to see you again soon!
