نبذة

Carv Hair Team Inc. stands out as a premier hair salon in Edmonton, specializing in a range of grooming services tailored for women and men. From rejuvenating scalp detoxes to precise fades or extensions, the skilled team ensures each client leaves feeling refreshed and confident. With convenient parking and a commitment to inclusivity, this salon offers an inviting atmosphere for everyone seeking top-notch hair care. Sit back, unwind, and enjoy a glass of wine or other beverage while we take care of your hair. Because your salon experience should be about more than beautiful hair — it should be a moment to relax, indulge, and leave feeling your absolute best. 🥂✨
