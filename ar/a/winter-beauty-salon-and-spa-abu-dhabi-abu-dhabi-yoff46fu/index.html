About

Winter Beauty Salon and Spa, located in the heart of Al Khalidiyah, Abu Dhabi, is your ultimate destination for relaxation and beauty. We offer a luxurious escape where beauty meets serenity, specializing in premium hair care, facials, nail services, Moroccan baths, and rejuvenating spa treatments. Our expert team is dedicated to enhancing your natural beauty and ensuring a personalized experience with every visit. Step into Winter Beauty Salon and Spa and indulge in a world of elegance, care, and relaxation, leaving you refreshed and radiant every time.
