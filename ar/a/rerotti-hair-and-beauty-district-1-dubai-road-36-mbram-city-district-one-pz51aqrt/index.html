About

At Rerotti beauty salon, founded in 1985, our commitment is to enhance your beauty comprehensively. With a stellar reputation, we offer a distinctive array of services, using globally acclaimed products. Our adept team of aestheticians ensures top-notch treatments for hair and beauty. Our modern, hygienic salon is equipped to provide a serene environment for relaxation and pampering. We aim to stay at the forefront of trends, delivering the latest and finest in hair, beauty, and products. Transforming your appearance, we aspire to make you not only look but also feel exquisitely beautiful.
