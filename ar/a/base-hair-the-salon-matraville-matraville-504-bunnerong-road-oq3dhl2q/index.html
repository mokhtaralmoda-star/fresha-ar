نبذة

Base Hair The Salon Matraville is a premium hair destination in Sydney’s Eastern Suburbs, specialising in luxury hair extensions, bespoke blonde transformations and expert hair colour. Our services include Tape Hair Extensions, Butterfly Weft, Keratin Bonds, Nano Bead Extensions, Balayage, Highlights, Global Colour, Nanoplasty and Hair Botox and more. Our experienced stylists combine technical expertise with a personalised approach to create beautiful, seamless results tailored to your individual style and hair goals. From dimensional blondes and rich, glossy colour to luxurious extensions and smoothing treatments, every service is designed to enhance your hair’s beauty, finish and feel. Enjoy $50 off your first appointment, complimentary consultations and the flexibility of Afterpay. We make achieving your dream hair feel effortless, with personalised recommendations, premium services and a welcoming salon experience. Our salon is easily accessible by public transport, with abundant free parking directly outside the salon. At Base Hair The Salon Matraville, we’re passionate about beautiful hair, premium results and helping you feel your absolute best!
