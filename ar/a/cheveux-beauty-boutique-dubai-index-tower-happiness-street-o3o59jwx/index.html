About

Cheveux Beauty Boutique embraces beauty and confidence. The main focus at Cheveux is our clients. When the salon chair turns, and the clients see themselves with their new hair, that smile drives us! We offer various services including hair coloring and styling, makeup, manicures, massages and so much more. Starting from Paris, Cheveux Beauty Boutique has extended to various other markets including London, Paris, and Dubai.
