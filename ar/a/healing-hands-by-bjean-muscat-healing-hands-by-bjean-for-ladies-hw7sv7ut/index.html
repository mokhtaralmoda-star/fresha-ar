About

Escape the stress of everyday life at Healing Hands by Bjean. Our ladies-only spa offers a premium wellness experience featuring Japanese-inspired Head Spa treatments, therapeutic massages, hot stone therapy, aromatherapy, foot reflexology, facial massage, and nail services. We believe every client deserves exceptional care and a memorable spa experience from the moment they arrive.
