نبذة

A place to unwind. We strive in creating a relaxing environment for all of our guests. A place where you can sit back and enjoy our service, invest in your beauty and health. The products used in our treatments are made of pure and natural ingredients, from ethical sources, including 3-free, 5-free, 7-free non-toxic nail polishes. We are fans of non-toxic, vegan and cruelty-free. We also love buying local. Our team personally sources new products produced locally that respect our high standards of quality. Lily and Roo is about more than just getting your nails done.
