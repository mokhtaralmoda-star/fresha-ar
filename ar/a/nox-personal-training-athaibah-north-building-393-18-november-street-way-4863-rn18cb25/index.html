About

NOX Personal Training is for individuals looking to achieve their fitness aspirations in a supportive environment. With a comprehensive array of services, from EMS training to personalized nutrition consultations, clients experience tailored programs that cater to their specific needs. This woman-owned studio embraces inclusivity, featuring amenities like showers and lockers, making it a convenient choice for all fitness enthusiasts. We offer packages with 8 or 12 sessions and each has specific validity period. Contact us to get more information.
