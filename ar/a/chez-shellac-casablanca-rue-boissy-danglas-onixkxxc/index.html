About

Chez Shellac is Morocco's first à la carte nail bar, set up in a private apartment by an independent nail technician and artist with over two years of experience. No queues, no waiting room, no rushing between clients. One artist and one client at a time, so the time is truly yours. Open every day from 12 PM to 12 AM, weekends and holidays included. Yes, midnight. You read that right. There are three ways to book. Ready to Wear sets are finished designs at one fixed price, with the base, manicure, color and finish already chosen for you. Build Your Own is for something more personal. Start from a base, choose each step, and watch the price update as you go. Choose from a growing collection of over 105 shades, with new additions every season. Nail health and hygiene are at the heart of how we work. We are Barbicide certified, we always keep it current, and we treat your natural nails as gently as we can. No hidden fees, you pay only for what you choose.
