About

Shehana World is a premier hair salon dedicated to enhancing beauty and confidence through expert care and high-quality products. With a focus on women’s services, they offer a range of treatments, including specialized shampoos and masks that nourish and revitalize hair. The salon prides itself on being woman-owned, providing a welcoming atmosphere for all clients, and is conveniently located near public transport with accessible parking.
