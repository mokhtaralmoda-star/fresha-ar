About

Nail Atelier Salon, located in Lamar Mall Jumeirah, offers an exquisite and luxurious beauty experience. Specializing in high-end nail care, the salon provides a wide range of services, including manicures, pedicures, nail art, and more. With a team of experienced and skilled professionals, Nail Atelier Salon is dedicated to enhancing the natural beauty of each client through personalized treatments. The salon prides itself on its relaxing atmosphere, using only the finest products to ensure exceptional quality.
