نبذة

Dealis Hair Boutique is a premium hairdresser in Manly, specialising in balayage, blonde transformations, brunette colouring and precision cutting. Founded by stylist Dario De Angelis, the salon blends Italian technique with modern, sustainable beauty. We are known as a blonde specialist in Manly, while also offering rich brunette colours, full colour services, colour correction, highlights and toning — all tailored to enhance your natural features. We also offer modern men’s haircuts and grooming, combining clean fades, scissor work and natural styling.
