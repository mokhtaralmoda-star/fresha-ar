نبذة

At Muscle Essence, the focus is on alleviating chronic pain and enhancing mobility through expert massage therapies and innovative body sculpting technology (Body Essence). Founded by experienced therapist Sophie K, the clinic offers a range of treatments, including Sports, Acupressure, Lymphatic, Pregnancy & Relaxation, tailored to individual needs. With options for Corporate massage packages and state-of-the-art equipment like the Sculpt X Plus machine, clients can expect a holistic approach to pain management and strength building, including technology for Pelvic floor strengthening.
