Wilde & Co hair artistry is a boutique salon located in Kingsford, we are a specialist blonde and balayage salon that have become known for our signature lived-in colours, beautiful blondes and gorgeous extensions. Our custom colour approach and fun chilled environment has established us a loyal tribe of Wilde babes that come back time and time again.
