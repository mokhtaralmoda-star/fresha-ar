About

Experience premium beauty at our newest location in Jabriya, Kuwait, nestled within a luxury hotel. As the second branch of a well-established salon, we offer a full range of professional services, including hair, nails, microblading, makeup, and more, all delivered with the highest standards of care and expertise. Indulge in a sophisticated atmosphere where every detail is tailored to enhance your beauty and well-being.
