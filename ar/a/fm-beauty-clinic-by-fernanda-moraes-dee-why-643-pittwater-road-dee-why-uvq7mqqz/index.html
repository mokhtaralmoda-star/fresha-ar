نبذة

FM Beauty is a boutique beauty studio in the heart of Dee Why, on Sydney’s Northern Beaches, specialising in brows, cosmetic tattooing, lash lifting and advanced skin treatments. With over 9 years of experience and hundreds of 5-star reviews, FM Beauty is known for natural, personalised results, exceptional attention to detail and a warm, welcoming experience. From expert brow shaping, tinting and lamination to cosmetic tattooing, lash lifts, facials, skin treatments and laser eyebrow tattoo removal, every treatment is tailored to enhance your natural beauty. Our philosophy is simple: no one-size-fits-all beauty. Every face, brow and skin is different, and every treatment should be too. ✨ Visit us in our beautiful new Dee Why studio and experience personalised beauty with care, expertise and attention to every detail.
