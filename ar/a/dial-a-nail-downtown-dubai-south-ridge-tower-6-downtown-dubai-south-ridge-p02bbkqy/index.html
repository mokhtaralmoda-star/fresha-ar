About

Since 2008, Dial a Nail has recognized the time constraints faced by today's women, unveiling a dual approach: a salon experience and home delivery service. With premium treatments spanning waxing, hair care, and nail art, our venture has burgeoned to 5 branches, earning acclaim as Dubai's premier beauty provider. Our commitment to impeccable hygiene and service ensures that every session becomes a cherished moment for our clients. Whether catering to housewives, students, or corporate professionals, Dial a Nail strives to offer every woman a well-deserved respite.
