نبذة

Located in the heart of Perth, Patron Barber redefines the modern grooming experience, blending timeless barbering traditions with contemporary sophistication. Our expert barbers deliver precision haircuts, seamless fades and refined beard grooming, with every detail carefully considered to ensure an exceptional experience. Recognised with the Outstanding Barbershop Award at the 2026 Local Business Awards, Patron Barber is built on a dedication to craftsmanship, impeccable service and uncompromising quality. From our carefully curated space to our personalised approach, every element reflects our commitment to excellence. More than a barbershop, Patron Barber is a destination for gentlemen who appreciate precision, individuality and understated luxury.
