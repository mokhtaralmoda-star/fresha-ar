نبذة

Posh N Polished is Melbourne's most luxurious nail and beauty clinic in Hawthorn, Melbourne, founded on the belief that exceptional beauty treatments begin with uncompromising standards. Every product is carefully researched for ingredient quality and proven performance, every technician is personally selected for their skill and professionalism, and every treatment is delivered with meticulous attention to detail and rigorous hygiene protocols. Specialising in Russian Manicures, Japanese Manicures, BIAB Nails, Gel-X extensions, Korean Lash Lifts, Threading, Brow Treatments and professional iS Clinical skincare, Posh N Polished combines advanced techniques with refined luxury to deliver beautiful, long-lasting results. More than a beauty salon, Posh N Polished is a place where clients can relax knowing every aspect of their experience has been thoughtfully considered—from clinical standards of cleanliness and premium products to personalised care and exceptional craftsmanship. The result is a luxury beauty experience that is elegant, trustworthy and designed to help every client look and feel their absolute best. Note: 12% surcharge applies to all services on Saturdays and PHs
