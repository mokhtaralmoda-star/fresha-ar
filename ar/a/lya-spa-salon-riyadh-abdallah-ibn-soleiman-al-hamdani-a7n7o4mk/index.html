About

Located in Riyadh, is a women’s beauty destination offering a range of services, including nails, hair treatments, and massages. Renowned as the first salon in Riyadh to provide the luxurious Tunisian bath experience, LYA Spa & Salon combines traditional relaxation techniques with modern beauty care, ensuring a rejuvenating and personalized experience in an elegant setting!
