About

Details Nails Artistry is a home-service spa created to elevate the modern beauty experience with exceptional Nail, Massage, Lashes, and Hair services delivered to your comfort place. We focus on simple, minimal, and detail-oriented care designed to help you relax, restore, and enjoy a memorable spa experience. Booking Policy: 1) Please share your location before booking to confirm coverage. 2) Minimum spend per specialist: SAR 185. Changes & Cancelations Policy: 1. A 100% deposit is required to confirm all bookings. 2. If a booking is cancelled or rescheduled 2 hours before the appointment, SAR 100 will be deducted as a fee. The remaining amount will be credited to your wallet for future bookings. 3. If a cancellation or reschedule occurs before 1 hour, the full service amount will be non-refundable. 4. If we arrive and the client is 15 minutes late or more, the appointment will be considered cancelled and the service amount will be non-refundable. 5. Once the service has been provided, the service amount is non-refundable.
