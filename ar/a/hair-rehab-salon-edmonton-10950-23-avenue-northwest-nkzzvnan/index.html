نبذة

Hair Rehab Salon is a healthy-hair focused salon in Southwest Edmonton, created to provide a calm, welcoming and personalized experience for every hair texture. Founded by licensed hairstylist Tusha with over 20 years of professional experience, the salon specializes in textured, curly, coily and straight hair, with services including Hair Botox, silk presses, precision cuts, colour, loc care, protective styling and more. Every service is approached with hair health, education and long-term care in mind. Come as you are, relax, and leave with hair that looks beautiful and feels healthier. Healthy Hair Starts Here -Repair. Restore. Protect.
