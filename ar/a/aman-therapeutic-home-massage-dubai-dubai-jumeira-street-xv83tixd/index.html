نبذة

Aman Therapeutic is a premium home massage and wellness service in Dubai, bringing a complete spa-quality experience directly to your home, hotel or villa. Our professional therapists arrive with everything needed for your session, including the massage bed, fresh linens, oils and treatment essentials. What makes Aman different is our personalized approach — with most massage styles, you can combine techniques based on what your body needs, whether you’re looking for relaxation, deeper pressure, stretching or recovery. Choose from relaxation, deep tissue, Swedish, sports, prenatal & postnatal, lymphatic drainage, Thai, Kobido, couples massage, hot stone, cupping, Maderotherapy and our signature Ethiopian massage experience. Your body, your massage. Enjoy professional wellness, thoughtful care and the comfort of staying home — plus our signature coffee body scrub with eligible sessions.
