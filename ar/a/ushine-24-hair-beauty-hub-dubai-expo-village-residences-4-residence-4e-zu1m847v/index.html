About

Ushine 24 Hair & Beauty Hub is the only salon in Expo City. It welcomes clients from Dubai South, Emaar South, Al Furjan, The Greens, Dubai Investments Park (DIP), Jebel Ali and nearby areas. Its services include expert acrylic and hard gel removal as well as scalp treatments, and each one is tailored to the client in a calm, relaxing space. The salon follows eco-friendly practices and offers easy parking and wheelchair access, so every visit is comfortable and convenient.
