About

Maggie Semaan, one of Dubai’s most renowned hair artists, brings over 20 years of global experience shaping trends and redefining beauty. A multi-award-winning stylist with more than ten first-place titles, Maggie’s visionary work has lit up international stages, graced the covers of leading fashion magazines, and earned the trust of A-list celebrities around the world. Now, she joins forces with Caroline Stanbury, English socialite, television personality, and businesswoman, to create a new destination of luxury and sophistication.
