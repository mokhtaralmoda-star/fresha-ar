About

At Lux Atila - JVC, every woman can indulge in a transformative experience tailored to enhance her natural beauty. Specializing in root color and ammonia-free options using premium brands like L'Oréal and Schwarzkopf, the salon also offers exquisite nail extensions in both acrylic and polygel. With a commitment to quality and a welcoming atmosphere, this woman-owned salon ensures that every visit is as empowering as it is luxurious.
