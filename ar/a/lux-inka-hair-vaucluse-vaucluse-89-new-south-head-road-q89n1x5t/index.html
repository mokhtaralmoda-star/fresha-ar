نبذة

Maria Salvador has been a fixture in the Eastern Suburbs community for over 25 years. She launched her first business, Just Inka Hair Salon, in 2008, and over the past 15 years, the salon has grown in tandem with her loyal client base. In 2018, Maria opened Lux Vaucluse, a new salon concept that was warmly received by the community. Maria is a passionate entrepreneur who is committed to providing her clients with the highest quality services. She is also a devoted mother and wife, and she is active in her community.
