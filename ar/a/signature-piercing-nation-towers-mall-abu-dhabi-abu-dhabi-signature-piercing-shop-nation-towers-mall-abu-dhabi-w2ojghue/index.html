About

SiSignature Piercing is the first licensed piercing center to open in Abu Dhabi. We proudly launched our new company in early 2023 as a sister establishment to “Dr. Aisha VIP Piercing Center” in Amman, Jordan. Since 2017, we have been dedicated to providing the highest quality professional piercing services and jewelry. Our customers recognize us as the cleanest and friendliest piercing center in Abu Dhabi. We have built a reputation for being at the forefront of high-quality body jewelry and piercing technology.
