About

In Oud Metha, The Loft Prime Gent's Salon stands as a distinguished haven for the modern gentleman, blending classic barbershop charm with progressive grooming techniques. Clients can indulge in a range of services, including relaxing back massages with Moroccan baths, meticulous manicures, and stylish haircuts paired with soothing face scrubs. This upscale barbershop is dedicated to providing a therapeutic yet masculine atmosphere, ensuring every visit leaves patrons feeling rejuvenated and impeccably groomed.
