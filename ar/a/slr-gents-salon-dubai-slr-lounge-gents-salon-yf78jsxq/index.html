About

Unleash your inner icon at SLR Lounge, a luxurious destination in Dubai’s Jaddaf Waterfront that combines beauty transformations with professional growth. This sophisticated space offers a top-tier salon experience, featuring haircuts, styling, skincare, and cosmetic treatments, alongside a curated cosmetic showroom. Aspiring stylists can perfect their craft at the on-site academy, guided by industry experts. Enjoy personalized pampering and leave feeling confident, beautiful, and empowered.
