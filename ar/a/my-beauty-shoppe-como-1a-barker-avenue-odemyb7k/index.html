نبذة

For many years, My Beauty Shoppe has been a well-known, trusted one-stop beauty destination for the local Como community. It has been a familiar place where clients can enjoy a wide range of beauty, skin and relaxation treatments conveniently under one roof. As the beauty industry continues to evolve, so do the techniques, products and standards required to provide truly exceptional results. Under new management, we’re building upon the salon’s established foundations while thoughtfully elevating every part of the client experience. This exciting transformation will introduce **COMO BEAUTY by Nuri Studio**—a premium boutique salon where every service is performed with care, attention to detail and a commitment to consistently high standards. Our focus is not simply to offer an extensive treatment menu, but to ensure every treatment is delivered using carefully selected, high-quality products and modern techniques. From lashes, brows and advanced facials to relaxing massage and vegan-friendly manicures, our services are designed to enhance your natural beauty while giving you the time and space to relax, recharge and feel genuinely cared for.
