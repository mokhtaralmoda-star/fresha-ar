نبذة

Cellulite reduction. Fluid retention. Body contouring. Skin toning. Improved tissue mobility. LPG Endermologie is a clinically studied, non-invasive mechanical massage technology, with scientific evidence supporting its use in the reduction of cellulite appearance. Using controlled rolling and suction, the technology creates targeted mechanical stimulation across the skin and underlying tissues. This action supports circulation and fluid movement while helping to mobilise the tissues, improve skin tone and refine the body’s natural contours. Every treatment is customised to your body, the areas being treated and your individual goals. THE CONTOUR METHOD EXPERIENCE Begin with a gentle vibration plate session, followed by your personalised LPG Endermologie treatment. Settle into calming music, subtle aromatherapy and a private, tranquil environment, with a cup of tea to enjoy before or after your treatment.
