About

At MIER BEAUTY LOUNGE, each treatment is designed to elevate the beauty experience in a tranquil, adults-only environment. Specializing in a diverse range of services, from nail extensions to System Professional treatments, this woman-owned salon places a premium on personalized care and quality.
