نبذة

Sweat & Chill – Your Coastal Space to Reset Tucked away in the chilled vibes of North Beach, Sweat & Chill is a private space to slow down, switch off and reconnect with yourself. Whether you’re here to sweat, plunge or simply shift your state, there’s more than one way to reset. 🧖‍♀️ Infrared Sauna + Ice Bath Our private recovery sessions pair a full-spectrum infrared barrel sauna (up to 70°C) with an icy 5–7°C plunge. Come solo or bring a mate and experience the simple power of hot + cold. ✨ The Shift A 60-minute, guided, multisensory sauna & ice bath experience, layered with frequency, breathwork, movement & sound. Spinal Energetics A 1:1 somatic modality that works with the nervous system and the spine to shift what the mind can't reach and talk therapy can't touch. Available in person or online. Come as you are. Leave feeling unstoppable
