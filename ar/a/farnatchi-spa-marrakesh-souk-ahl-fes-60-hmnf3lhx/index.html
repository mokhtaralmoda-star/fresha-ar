About

Farnatchi Spa is a beautifully designed day spa in the historic heart of the Marrakech medina. Stunning private marble hammams with vaulted ceilings, restful treatment rooms with their own shower facilities and a menu of light healthy lunches, ensure that the pampering doesn’t stop with our world class therapists Our Spa in Marrakech now offers the best facilities and best facilities need the best products. We have teamed up with an internationally acclaimed luxury spa brand and Morocco’s finest specialist in traditional products, to offer our clients the very best selection possible.
