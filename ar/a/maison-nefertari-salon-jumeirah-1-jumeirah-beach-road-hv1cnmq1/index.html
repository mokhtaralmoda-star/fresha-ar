نبذة

Upon stepping into Maison Nefertari, you are entering a sacred Temple. Every gesture, ingredient element and rituals is inspired by the monst monumental civilisation, Egypt. Your journey will commence with a welcome ritual that will enable you to dive into an instant state of well-being that will ignite sensorial textures, mystical, ancient melodires and organic elixirs inspired by Ancient Egyptian traditions. Like the world of Maison Nefertari, we aim for our spa to emit authenticity and we favour creating a harmonious synergy between our guests and Swnw's (Su-nu).
