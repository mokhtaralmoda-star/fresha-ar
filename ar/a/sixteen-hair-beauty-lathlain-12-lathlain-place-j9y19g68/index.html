نبذة

Sixteen Hair & Beauty is the reimagined home of Nuova U Salon. Still known for exceptional hair, we’ve evolved into a full hair and beauty destination in the heart of Lathlain. Located upstairs with beautiful city views, our space is designed to feel earthy, relaxed and welcoming. While hair remains at the heart of everything we do, we now offer a curated range of beauty services including facials, massage, nails, waxing and cosmetic tattooing. Sixteen is a space where great hair, beauty and a warm atmosphere come together. Bring your bestie, settle in and leave feeling better than ever.
