نبذة

Established in 2008 in Markham, Oakspa Wellness is a client-focused wellness spa offering a clean, cozy, and welcoming environment. Every visit is designed to help you relax, rejuvenate, and feel your best. With attention to detail and a commitment to well-being, Oakspa provides a serene space where comfort and care take center stage. We proudly offer Registered Massage Therapy (RMT) services, many of which may be covered by extended health insurance plans, along with a thoughtfully curated collection of signature wellness treatments. From our popular Couples Massage Bundle and Facelift Massage to Brazilian Lymphatic Massage and Scalp Revival, each service is designed to relieve tension, restore balance, and support your overall well-being—because at Oakspa, therapy meets wellbeing.
