نبذة

Located in Citizen Salon Studios. Go to building 3211 A, 4th floor, then studio #35, left hand Hallway. Come see us in South Surrey, BC for your next cut! At The Velvet Barber, it’s more than just a haircut, it’s a place to sit back, relax, and leave feeling fresh. Our owner-operated barbershop offers tailored men’s haircuts, elegant natural styles, beard grooming, and clean-ups, with every cut customized to the individual. We take pride in quality work, attention to detail, and making every client feel comfortable and taken care of. We use premium natural hair products and maintain high standards of service to provide a quality barbershop experience from start to finish.
