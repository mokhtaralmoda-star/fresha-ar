About

The Star Kids Australian Salon in Dubai Festival City Mall offers a delightful experience tailored for children. From the moment kids walk in, they are greeted by a vibrant, playful atmosphere designed to make them feel comfortable and excited. The salon is brightly decorated with kid-friendly themes, featuring fun, colorful chairs shaped like cars and airplanes. Professional stylists, who specialize in working with children, ensure that every haircut or styling session is both enjoyable and stress-free.
