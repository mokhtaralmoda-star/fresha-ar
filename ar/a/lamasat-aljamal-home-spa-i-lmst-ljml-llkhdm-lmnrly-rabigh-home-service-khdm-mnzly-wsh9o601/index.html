About

At Lamasat Aljamal, we bring beauty to your doorstep—because self-care should be convenient, luxurious, and tailored to you. Based in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, our home service nail salon is dedicated to providing high-quality nail care in the comfort and privacy of your home. Our experienced technicians specialize in a full range of nail services, including classic and gel manicures, pedicures, nail art, and spa treatments. With a strong focus on hygiene, professionalism, and customer satisfaction, we ensure every appointment feels like a pampering escape.
