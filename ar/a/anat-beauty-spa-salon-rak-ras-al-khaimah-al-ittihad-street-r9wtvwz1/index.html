About

Ras Al Khaimah’s ultimate self-care sanctuary for ladies, where beauty, wellness, and relaxation come together under one roof. At Anat, we believe self-care is more than a routine, it’s a moment to pause, unwind, and reconnect with yourself. From the moment our guests step inside, they are welcomed into a serene and elegant atmosphere thoughtfully designed to offer an escape from the everyday. Our extensive range of services brings together everything you need for a complete head-to-toe experience, from personalized hair and nail care to relaxing massages, Moroccan hammam, lashes, makeup, waxing, and more. Each treatment is delivered by our skilled and dedicated team with attention to detail, personalized care, and a passion for enhancing natural beauty. We continuously evolve with the latest beauty and wellness treatments while upholding the high standards Anat is known for, ensuring every visit leaves our guests feeling beautifully cared for, relaxed, refreshed, and renewed.
