About

Conveniently located in One Deira Mall, Matinee Gents Salon is one of the best men's salons in Bur Dubai. Our clients are pampered with a full range of hair, skin, and nail services. The team of stylists at Matinee Gents Salon provides a range of grooming services, from bespoke haircuts and blow-dries to mani-pedis and beard trims. Waxing and threading treatments are also available, alongside facials designed to refresh complexions. Visit us to have your grooming services.
