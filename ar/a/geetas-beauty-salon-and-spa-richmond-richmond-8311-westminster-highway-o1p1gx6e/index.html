نبذة

With a legacy that began in 2005, Geeta’s Beauty Salon and Spa has become a cherished destination for beauty enthusiasts in Richmond. Renowned for its commitment to quality and customer satisfaction, the salon specializes in a variety of waxing services, utilizing only organic and vegan products. As a woman-owned establishment, Geeta’s prioritizes environmentally friendly practices while providing a welcoming atmosphere, complete with convenient amenities like parking and wheelchair accessibility.
