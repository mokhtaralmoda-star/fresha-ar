نبذة

STRETCHED at Ripe Market — our air-conditioned pop-up studio inside Dubai Police Academy Park. Experience personalised 1:1 assisted stretching in a calm, comfortable space. You simply lie down and relax while one of our trained stretchers guides your body through stretches tailored to your needs. Whether you spend long hours at a desk, lead an active lifestyle or simply need time to unwind, our sessions focus on improving flexibility, easing muscle tension and helping you move more freely. Choose a full-body session or a shorter, focused session to give specific areas extra attention. With relaxing music and our signature scent, it’s a moment to slow down and reconnect with your body.
