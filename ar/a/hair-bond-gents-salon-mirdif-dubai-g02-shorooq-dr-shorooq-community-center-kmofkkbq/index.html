About

HairBond Gent's Salon is one of Dubai's top-rated barbershops, conveniently located in Shoorooq Community Center, Mirdif. Our skilled and well-trained staff offers a wide range of professional grooming services, including gents cuts, shaving, blow-drying, hair treatments, and facial scrubs. We pride ourselves on delivering exceptional quality and a luxurious experience, ensuring every client leaves feeling refreshed and confident. Whether you're looking for a classic haircut or a modern style, HairBond is your go-to destination for all your grooming needs in Dubai.
