نبذة

Founded in 2019, we are a traditional barbershop with a modern twist, located in the heart of Fremantle. Independently owned and operated by two friends. Dylen has over 18 years of experience in the barber industry, with multiple awards won for his talent. Hayden has experience in design and marketing, bringing all merch and culture from Bosuns to life. We want to create a sense of community within our four walls that connects with locals and businesses in and around the area. We aren’t just a barber shop — we are a hub for Fremantle, bringing people closer together.
