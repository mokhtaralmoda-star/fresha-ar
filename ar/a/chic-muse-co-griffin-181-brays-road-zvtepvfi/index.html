نبذة

Chic Muse Co. - a cozy home-based brow and lash studio in Griffin. With over 3 years of industry experience, including 2 years in an exclusive brow salon, I specialise in Brow Laminations, Brow Sculpt & Dye, and Korean Lash Lifts tailored to enhance your natural features. As a Diploma-qualified Beauty Therapist with advanced training in Brow Laminations, Korean Lash Lifts, and Colour Theory, I combine professional expertise with a personalised approach. I'm passionate about creating beautiful, consistent results while ensuring every client feels comfortable, confident, and cared for throughout their appointment. One of the things I'm most proud of is the strong relationships I've built with my clients. Many have followed me throughout my journey and continue to trust me as their dedicated brow and lash artist. At Chic Muse Co. you'll enjoy a welcoming one-on-one experience, complimentary refreshments, flexible appointment times, free visitor parking, and aftercare packs with every lamination treatment. I look forward to helping you achieve your dream brows and lashes.
