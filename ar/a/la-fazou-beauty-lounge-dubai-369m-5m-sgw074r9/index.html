About

Situated in the refined Arjan district of Dubai, La Fazou offers a haven of elegance and sophistication for those who seek the ultimate in beauty care. At La Fazou, every service from bespoke hair styling and color treatments to luxurious facials, meticulous manicures, and our exclusive Moroccan pedicure and manicure—is crafted to enhance natural beauty while providing a serene escape. Our team of highly skilled professionals brings a wealth of expertise, using the latest techniques and the finest products to ensure that each visit leaves clients feeling renewed and confident.
