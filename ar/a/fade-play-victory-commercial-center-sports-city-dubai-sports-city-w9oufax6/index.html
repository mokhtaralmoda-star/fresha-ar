About

Fade & Play is a top-rated barbershop near JVC, Motor City, Victory Heights, Dubai Sports City, DubaiLand, and Arabian Ranches, known for precision barbering and premium grooming services. Located at Victory Commercial Center in Dubai Sports City, our experienced barbers specialize in skin fades, precision haircuts, beard sculpting, Afro hair, braids, and dreadlocks. We also offer grooming treatments including facials, manicure, and pedicure. With skilled barbers, a great atmosphere, and consistent quality, Fade & Play is trusted by clients across the area for sharp cuts and professional work.
