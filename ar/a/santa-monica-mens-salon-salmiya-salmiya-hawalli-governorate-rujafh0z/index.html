About

At Santa Monica - Men's Salon, men can experience a tranquil escape while receiving exceptional grooming services tailored specifically for them. The skilled team offers advanced treatments such as the Medical Pedicure and Titanium Thread Treatment, ensuring each visit is both rejuvenating and effective. With convenient amenities like parking and proximity to public transport, this adults-only sanctuary is dedicated to enhancing personal care in a sophisticated environment.
