About

The Barber Room offers a full service barbershop in Dubai. Our services are exclusive for boys and gents by licensed hairdressers, in addition to the best quality hair cut services that are delivered by our experienced barbers. The Barber Room also offers hair colouring, cutting, highlighting, shampooing, facial waxing, beard trims, and professional manicures and pedicures by trained therapists. At The Barber Room, you can escape the stress of everyday life with quality service offered at the right price.
