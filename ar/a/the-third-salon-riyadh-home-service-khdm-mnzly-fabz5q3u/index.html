About

At THIRD, premium at-home nail care is done as it should be with thoughtful hygiene, honest care and quality products in every detail. Booking Policy A non-refundable 50% deposit secures your appointment. Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance. Changes within 24 hours are charged in full. If you are unavailable for 15 minutes after your specialist arrives, it is considered a no-show and charged in full. Please check your nails before your specialist leaves. Completed services are non-refundable.
