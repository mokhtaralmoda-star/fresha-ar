نبذة

MARA.H Skin & Aesthetics is a founder-led medical aesthetics clinic in Chatswood, Sydney, specialising in Asian and multicultural skin (Fitzpatrick III–V) for over 15 years. Founded by ASPA Gold Award winner Marina Hu, we are an authorised Cynosure PicoSure Pro, Merz Ultherapy Prime, DMK, and INDIBA Pro Max clinic. All prescription treatments are assessed and prescribed by Ahpra-registered medical practitioners and delivered by our clinical team of registered nurses. We offer bilingual English and Chinese service, including WeChat consultations. Google 5.0 rating. Located in the heart of Chatswood, walking distance from Westfield and Chatswood Chase. Free consultation available for first-time clients.
