About

An ocean of passion, love, and purpose has gone into founding **Weamour**—*Woven by Love, Rooted in Sovereignty*. Sixteen years ago, I found myself at a crossroads. I had lost everything and had no idea where to turn or what my next step would be. In that moment of uncertainty, a dear friend reached out and gently guided me toward my first healing experience. That single step became the beginning of a profound journey of self-discovery and transformation. As I immersed myself in these experiences, I witnessed deep emotional shifts within myself that helped me heal, reconnect, and rediscover my inner strength. Those life-changing experiences ignited a calling to support others on their own healing journeys, inspiring me to dedicate myself to this deeply transformative work. Weamour was born from that journey—a space woven with love and rooted in the belief that every person has the innate capacity to heal, reclaim their sovereignty, and thrive.
