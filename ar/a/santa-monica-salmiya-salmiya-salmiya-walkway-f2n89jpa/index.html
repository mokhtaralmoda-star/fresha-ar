About

In the heart of Salmiya, this hair salon redefines beauty with a focus on exquisite nail services. Specialties like Rubber Base French and dazzling Rubber Base Glitter enhance every manicure, while crystal stones add a touch of glamour to your look. Catering exclusively to women and offering a kid-friendly environment, clients can enjoy a relaxing experience with convenient parking and access to public transport.
