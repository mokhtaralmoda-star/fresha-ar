نبذة

Corner Palace is a stylish and upscale barbershop in Riyadh, offering a modern yet comfortable atmosphere. It provides a range of grooming services, including haircuts, shaves, beard trims, and specialized treatments. Known for its skilled barbers and premium products, the shop combines luxury with classic barbering, ensuring a high-quality, personalized experience for every client. It's an ideal spot for men looking to refresh their style in a sophisticated setting.
