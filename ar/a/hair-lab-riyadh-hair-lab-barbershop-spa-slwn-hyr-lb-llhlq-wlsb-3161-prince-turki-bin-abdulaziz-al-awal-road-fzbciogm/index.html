About

Hair Lab is a high-end barbershop and spa for men in Riyadh, offering a blend of classic grooming services and luxurious spa treatments. With skilled barbers and a stylish, minimalist interior, it specializes in haircuts, beard trims, facials, massages, and other rejuvenating services. The shop provides a relaxed, upscale experience, making it the go-to spot for modern men seeking quality grooming and self-care.
