نبذة

The Barber Blueprint is based in Nundah, Brisbane. We set out to create a modern and sleek barbershop that offered those traditional barber services because we saw the need for a progression in men’s grooming and styling. We believe gentlemen should receive the best possible experience when they visit a barber. We take traditional barber qualities to create current and on-trend styles for the modern gent. Our experienced, passionate barbers will work to understand your needs and provide personalised grooming, so it’s tailored to you.
