About

Raynor Massage & Bodywork with Charles Aclottey. An officially registered company of the World Massage Federation (Protocol No. 1493). Charles Aclottey is an officially registered member of the World Massage Federation (ID: 26/0453) and holds a WMF Athletic Pass (2027). Operating under Trendyman Grooming and Wellness FZE LLC. Raynor Massage is a holistic deep-tissue system that works through tension bands to address chronic tightness, restricted mobility, and athletic recovery. Whether you are recovering from a marathon, intense gym sessions, or desk-job tension, each session is tailored to your body's specific needs—not a standard spa routine.
