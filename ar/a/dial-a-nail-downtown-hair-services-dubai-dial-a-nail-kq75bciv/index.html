About

The modern woman is always on the move and doesn't have enough “me time” as much as she would like to. That's why we have introduced premium beauty salons and home service delivery established since 2008. We offer a wide variety of quality treatments, from hair care to nail care and all beauty services in between; there's truly something for everyone. 13 years and 8 branches later, Dial a Nail is now one of Dubai's most trusted full-fledge beauty providers. We strive to meet every woman's beauty needs at the luxury of her own time, whether at home or in one of our boutique salons.
