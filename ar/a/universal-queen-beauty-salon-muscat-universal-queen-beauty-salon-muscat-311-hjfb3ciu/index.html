About

Universal Queen Beauty Salon stands as a sanctuary for women seeking a transformative beauty experience in Muscat. With a range of specialized services, including the trendy Butterfly Cut and soothing body massages, clients can indulge in pampering tailored to their needs. The salon also offers convenient parking and a welcoming atmosphere, making it an ideal destination for relaxation and self-care.
