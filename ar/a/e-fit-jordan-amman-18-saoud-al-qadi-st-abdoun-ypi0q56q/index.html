About

E-Fit is the world’s most advanced EMS Technology as it’s the safest, most comfortable and most effective. E-Fit is the world’s only FDA Approved Full-Body EMS Technology. At E-Fit Jordan, we offer a wide range of services specialized and focused on the human health & wellness. Our services are tailored to individual goals, needs and physical conditions. Our premium services include: E-Fit Fitness EMS: Active Full-Body 20-minutes training for for fat loss, muscle building, general strengthening and more. E-Fit Beauty & Slim EMS: Passive 20-minutes treatments (without exercising) for shaping & toning, cellulite fighting, skin tightening and more. E-Fit Medical & Rehabilitation EMS: Active and/or Passive 20-minutes treatment sessions for recovery of injuries and rehabilitation. E-Pilates: Active Full-Body 20-minutes training on Reformer Pilates combined with the E-Fit Fitness EMS Technology Conventional Physiotherapy Conventional Personal Training Clinical Nutrition Blood & DNA testing services Wellness services and assessments
