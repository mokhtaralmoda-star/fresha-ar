About

Shea Beauty Center and Spa is a well-established ladies’ beauty and wellness destination in Qatar, offering a relaxing environment where clients can enjoy professional beauty, grooming, and self-care services. It has built a strong local reputation. Shea Spa focuses on combining beauty, relaxation, and personalized care, providing clients with an all-in-one destination for their regular beauty routine may it be for hair, nails, massage, slimming, Hammam Rituals & much more.
