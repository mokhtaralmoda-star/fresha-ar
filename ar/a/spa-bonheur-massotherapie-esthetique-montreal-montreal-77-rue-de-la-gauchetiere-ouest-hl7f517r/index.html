نبذة

Located in the heart of Downtown Montreal’s Chinatown, Spa Bonheur offers professional massage therapy and skincare in a calm, welcoming environment. Just steps from Place-d'Armes Metro, the Palais des congrès de Montréal, and Old Montreal, we are conveniently located for local residents, office professionals, business travelers, and visitors.
